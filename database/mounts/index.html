Mounts

Horses, mount appearances and mounted open-world traversal.

How Mounts Work

Mounts provide faster ground traversal across large regions and are especially useful before every Boundary Stone is activated. Mount appearance items can change the visual style without replacing your broader progression.

When to Use a Mount

  • Long road travel between unexplored subregions
  • Regional quest routes before fast travel is unlocked
  • Collectible loops across flat/open terrain

Mount Appearances

New mount appearances are regularly added through patches, events, shops and seasonal reward lines. This hub is separated from combat gear so cosmetic mount updates can be added without touching equipment pages.
