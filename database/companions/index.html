Companions

NPC and companion assistance for exploration and selected boss encounters.

Combat Use

Some encounters allow you to bring NPCs or Companions. They can draw aggro, deal damage or provide healing, making difficult world bosses more manageable.

Version 1.8

The Companions Make Home era expanded home and companion-focused systems, including cozy life features and animal companions.

Tip

Use Companions to stabilize first clears, then remove them later if you want to practice boss patterns solo.
