NPCs

Interactable characters, AI-chat friendships, quest givers and progression NPCs.

NPC Systems

Some NPCs can be befriended through AI Chat, while others serve as quest givers, Wayfarers, merchants, sect contacts or progression specialists.

Important NPC Roles

RoleWhat They Do
WayfarersReveal portions of regional maps.
Sect ContactsStart initiation and faction progression.
MerchantsSell supplies, currencies and specialized goods.
Oddity ContactsAccept regional collectibles and unlock rewards.
AI NPCsSupport conversation-based friendship interactions.

Known Examples

Qi Sheng is important to Qinghe Oddity progression; Han Yanluo handles Masked Troupe initiation; Icchantika is central to Midnight Blades initiation. Later regions add many additional interactable NPCs.
