Quest Type

Side Quests

Exploration quests, encounters, Worldly Affairs and other optional story chains.

Why Complete Them

Side quests can reward Mystic Skills, medicine/lore records, currencies, NPC progress and access to additional world systems.

Recommended Method

Finish side quests while you are still clearing the surrounding subregion. This minimizes backtracking and often chains naturally into nearby exploration objectives.
