Early Campaign

Qinghe begins with Chapter 1: Heaven Has No Pier. Kaifeng continues through Chapter 2: Universal Furnace. Later updates expand the story through the western regions, Imperial Palace and Hidden Mountain.

Why Side Quests Matter

Side content frequently unlocks Mystic Skills, exploration mechanics, items, NPC relationships and regional lore, so it should not be treated as purely optional filler.
