Raging Tides

A later western sect introduced with the Liangzhou-era global content.

Identity

Raging Tides is themed around wandering warriors, drink and a rougher western-Jianghu atmosphere.

When to Join

Its content belongs to later progression than the launch-region sects, so expect to reach the corresponding western story and map content first.

Build Connection

Liangzhou also introduced later weapon/path content such as Stonesplit - Strength, making the region important for heavy melee progression.
