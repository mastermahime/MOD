Sect

Well of Heaven

A chivalry- and brotherhood-focused sect associated with defensive frontline combat.

Values

Well of Heaven emphasizes chivalrous deeds, generosity and cooperation. Members are expected to avoid harming fellow disciples.

Signature Martial Art

Thundercry Blade is associated with the sect and fits its sturdy frontline identity.

Best For

A strong fit for players who like co-op, tankier builds and faction rules that reward supportive play.
