Silver Needles

A healer-oriented sect suited to players who enjoy restoring allies and support-focused combat.

Role

Silver Needles is the natural faction choice for dedicated healers and players who prefer group sustain over pure damage.

Combat Connection

The sect is closely associated with healing-oriented fan gameplay, making Panacea Fan a natural fit for its identity.

Best For

Group PvE, support builds, guild activities and players who want their faction goals to align with healing.
