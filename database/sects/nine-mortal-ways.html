Sect

Nine Mortal Ways

A deceptive, trickery-focused sect connected to alternate initiation routes and the Mortal Rope Dart.

How to Join

The sect can be reached through overlapping initiation routes involving the Well of Heaven or Midnight Blades clues. The sect system becomes available from level 10.

Signature Combat

Mortal Rope Dart is closely associated with Nine Mortal Ways and can be obtained through its initiation path.

Playstyle

Best suited to players who enjoy unconventional objectives, deception-themed faction flavor and fast Rope Dart combat.
