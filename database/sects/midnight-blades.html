Midnight Blades

A combat-first faction that rewards PvP activity and grants access to Infernal Twinblades.

How to Join

At level 10 or higher, follow the clue toward Halo Peak and the injured swordsman. The initiation revolves around receiving a decree and staining it through a kill before returning to Icchantika.

Signature Martial Art

Joining grants access to Infernal Twinblades, a fast multi-hit style commonly paired with Mortal Rope Dart.

Weekly Identity

The faction strongly favors PvP. Karmic progression and activities such as Perception Forest align with its aggressive rules.

Leaving

Leaving a sect uses Betray Master and may require a Severance Trial before the process is fully cleared.
