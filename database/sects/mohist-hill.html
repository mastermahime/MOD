Mohist Hill

A Mohist faction/theme connected to Hidden Mountain and Version 2.x worldbuilding.

Theme

Mohist Hill is associated with universal love, mutual benefit, engineering and the clockwork culture at the heart of Hidden Mountain.

Version 2.x

Hidden Mountain and Mohist Hill are central to the 2.0–2.1 era. New Inner Ways and combat progression increasingly reference Mohist locations and systems.
