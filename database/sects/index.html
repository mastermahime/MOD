How Sects Work

Sects are rule-based factions. You normally need to reach the sect system, obtain or follow its clue, complete an initiation, and then maintain sect values through weekly behavior. Only one sect can be active at a time.

Why Join?

  • Sect shops and exclusive cosmetics
  • Reputation ranks and weekly progression
  • Access or discounts for signature Martial Arts
  • Faction activities, elections, trials or PvP objectives
Availability changes as new global updates release. Pages marked as later-release factions are kept separate so their join requirements can be updated without changing this index.
