Performance Sect

Masked Troupe

A performance-focused faction where members adopt stage identities and build popularity.

How to Join

At level 10 or higher, the sect clue leads to Fairgrounds in Kaifeng. The Online-mode initiation involves speaking with Han Yanluo, performing music or dance, gaining a Like, and returning to finish the initiation.

Progression

Members rise through Virtuoso-style ranks, gain access to a sect shop, and participate in weekly voting and performance objectives.

Weekly Goal

The sect emphasizes Performer Popularity, Spotlight Mode and published performances.
