Sect

Velvet Shade

A charm- and social-themed faction associated with the Vernal Umbrella.

Values

Velvet Shade's identity revolves around charm, affection and social interaction rather than straightforward military discipline.

Signature Martial Art

Vernal Umbrella is associated with the sect and supports ranged, elegant Silkbind play.

Best For

Players who enjoy social systems, ranged combat and a faction theme built around style rather than raw force.
