Item Type

Quick Use Items

Immediate-use healing, utility and activity items.

Examples

  • Yin-Yang Ointment
  • Hemostatic Powder
  • Fire Oil
  • Aqua Wax
  • Toxic Powder
  • Kid's Fishing Rod

Use

Quick-use items are meant for immediate overworld or activity effects. Version 1.7 also added auto-use settings for selected buff food and scrolls in modes such as Hero's Realm, Arena and Guild War.
