Offensive Weapon & Accessory Set

Starweave / Stars Align

Stacking Martial Art Skill set that rewards hitting bosses, players or multiple enemies while maintaining distance and avoiding damage.
Set Type: Offensive Weapon & Accessory Set
Equips in your weapon slots, Disc and Pendant.

Set Bonuses

2 PiecesMax Physical Attack +1
4 PiecesWhen hitting at least 2 enemies simultaneously, or hitting a boss or player, gain 1 stack of Starweave. For 5 seconds, Martial Art Skills deal 3% increased damage and gain further bonus damage against enemies more than 4 meters away, scaling with distance up to an additional 1% at 8 meters. Starweave stacks up to 5 times. Taking damage removes 1 stack. Up to 2 stacks per second can be gained.

Short Description

Starweave rewards ranged pressure, multi-target hits and boss aggression while punishing sloppy positioning. Maintaining stacks is easiest when you can keep distance and avoid chip damage.

Commonly Used Weapons

Inkwell Fan + Vernal UmbrellaVernal Umbrella (Main) + Inkwell FanMortal Rope Dart + Vernal Umbrella
Weapon recommendations are optional.

How to Obtain

Focus on Sword Trials, Hero's Realm and Outposts. There is no targeted campaign boss drop listed for this set.

Ideal Usage

Best for ranged DPS, aerial pressure and crowd-control builds that can repeatedly hit bosses or groups without taking return damage. Fan and Umbrella setups are especially comfortable because they can build stacks from range while preserving the 5-second windows.
