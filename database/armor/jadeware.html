Jadeware Set

Affinity-focused Martial Art burst set that rewards attacking controlled or low-Qi targets.
Set Type: Offensive Weapon & Accessory Set
Equips in your weapon slots, Disc and Pendant.

Set Bonuses

2 PiecesMax Physical Attack +27
4 PiecesCasting a Martial Art Skill activates Jadeware for 10 seconds: increases Affinity DMG by 10% and increases Direct Affinity Rate by 7.5% against targets in Qi Imbalance, targets whose Qi percentage is lower than yours, or targets below 40% Qi. This effect can trigger once every 12 seconds.

Short Description

Jadeware is easy to maintain in structured rotations and becomes especially strong when your build repeatedly creates Qi Imbalance or other low-Qi windows.

Ideal Usage

Best for technical but well-rounded PvE or PvP builds that cast Martial Art Skills frequently, manage enemy Qi and capitalize on short burst windows. It pairs especially well with Formbend, which supplies defensive value while Jadeware handles the offensive side of the setup.
