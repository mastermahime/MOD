Defensive Gear Set · Version 2.1

Brimflow

A defensive Special Enhancement set that gets sturdier as you absorb pressure during the empowered window.
2-PieceIncreases Physical Defense.
4-PieceTaking damage during Martial Art Special Enhancement builds damage-reduction stacks up to five. At maximum stacks, incoming Critical and Affinity damage is further reduced.
Best FitFrontline or Draught setups that expect to trade during enhancement.

Ideal Usage

Brimflow is useful when you cannot reliably avoid all incoming hits during an empowered rotation. It trades some offensive ceiling for more consistency and can help keep high-commitment melee sequences from being punished as hard.

Patch Note

Exact percentages can change. Compare your current in-game tooltip before deciding between Brimflow and older defensive sets.
