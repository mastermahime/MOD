Flawless Defense Set

A universal mitigation set that gives reliable passive damage reduction and becomes increasingly durable as your HP drops below 60%.
Set Type: Defensive Armor Set
Equips in the Helm, Chest Armor, Bracers and Greaves slots.

Set Bonuses

2 PiecesPhysical Defense +1
4 PiecesReduces damage taken by 5%. When HP falls below 60%, damage taken is further reduced by 1% for every 10% HP lost, stacking up to 5 times.

Short Description

Flawless Defense is an easy-to-use tank set with no complicated trigger requirement. It starts with a flat 5% damage reduction and becomes tougher as HP drops, reaching up to an additional 5% reduction at low health.

Ideal Usage

Flawless Defense provides passive tankiness that ramps as your HP falls, making it dependable in drawn-out PvE fights where specialized defensive triggers are not guaranteed. It is especially useful for players who want survivability without relying on Perfect Dodge, deflection, shields or other precise conditions.

Because the set becomes stronger below 60% HP, it works well for tank/DPS hybrids that can safely fight at reduced health while maintaining pressure.

Recommended Pairing

Pairing Flawless Defense with Moonflare-style weapon setups and weapons such as Heavenquaker Spear or Nameless Spear creates a durable frontline setup for co-op dungeons, bosses and sustained PvE.

Best fit: straightforward tank builds, low-HP sustain, long boss fights and players who want always-on mitigation.
