Mistwillow / Veil of the Willow

Attack-alternation set commonly referenced as Veil of the Willow in older/global community material.
2-Piece ThemePrecision Rate
4-PieceLight/Airborne Light attacks empower Heavy/Airborne Heavy damage for a short window, and Heavy-type attacks similarly empower the opposite attack family.
Best FitInkwell Fan / Vernal Umbrella and other rotations that naturally alternate attack types.

Ideal Use

Do not spam one button family. The set pays off when you deliberately alternate Light and Heavy/Charged sequences.
