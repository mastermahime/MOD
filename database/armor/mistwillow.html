Mistwillow / Veil of the Willow

Combo-focused Silkbind set that rewards alternating Heavy/Charged attacks with Light Attacks and Ballistic Skills.
Set Type: Offensive Weapon & Accessory Set
Equips in your weapon slots, Disc and Pendant.

Set Bonuses

2 PiecesPrecision Rate +0.1%
4 PiecesLanding a Heavy Attack or Heavy Attack Charged Skill increases the Physical and Silkbind damage of Light Attacks and Ballistic Skills by 10% for 15 seconds, and vice versa. When both bonuses are active at the same time, they upgrade to Mistwillow, increasing the Physical and Silkbind damage of both attack groups. Hitting with the corresponding skills can refresh the duration; refreshes can occur once every 2 seconds.

Short Description

Mistwillow is built for combo-heavy rotations and is especially comfortable on Fan/Umbrella setups that can chain safe ranged pressure, airborne sequences and alternating Light/Heavy inputs.

Commonly Used Weapons

Inkwell Fan + Vernal UmbrellaVernal Umbrella (Main) + Inkwell FanInfernal Twinblades + Strategic SwordStrategic Sword + Heavenquaker Spear
Weapon recommendations are optional; the set works with any rotation that can alternate the required attack types consistently.

Ideal Usage

Best for precision-oriented DPS builds that deliberately weave Light/Ballistic and Heavy/Charged attacks instead of spamming a single attack family. Inkwell Fan and Vernal Umbrella are natural fits because their ranged and airborne strings make it easier to keep both halves of the buff active.
