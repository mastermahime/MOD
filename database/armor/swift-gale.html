Swift Gale Set

Airborne Heavy Attack set built for launch, juggle and ranged aerial pressure.
Set Type: Offensive Weapon & Accessory Set
Equips in your weapon slots, Disc and Pendant.

Set Bonuses

2 PiecesMax Physical Attack +1
4 PiecesIncreases Airborne Heavy Attack damage by 10% and knocks the target down. The Knocked-Down effect can trigger once every 10 seconds.

Short Description

Swift Gale rewards launch-and-juggle rotations. Fan launches and Umbrella pressure make it easier to repeatedly land airborne Heavy Attacks and keep the set relevant throughout a fight.

Commonly Used Weapons

Inkwell Fan + Vernal UmbrellaVernal Umbrella (Main) + Inkwell FanMortal Rope Dart + Vernal Umbrella
Weapon recommendations are optional.

How to Obtain

Focus on Sword Trials, Hero's Realm and Outposts. There is no targeted campaign boss drop listed for this set.

Ideal Usage

Best for ranged DPS and crowd-control builds that can consistently put enemies airborne. It pairs naturally with Mistwillow / Veil of the Willow for airborne combo pressure, or with Beyond the Chill when you want a safer defensive package.
