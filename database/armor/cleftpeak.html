Set Bonuses

2 PiecesIncreased Min Physical Attack
4 PiecesHitting a boss or player grants Cleftpeak for 5 seconds: increases HP damage dealt by 1%, stacking up to 5 times. At 5 stacks, damage of Light/Heavy Attack Varied Combo, Assist Trigger Skill and assist effects is increased by a further 8%. A successful Deflect immediately grants 5 stacks.

Enhanced Skills

The additional damage boost applies to the Light/Heavy Attack Varied Combos of Snowparting Blade, Thundercry Blade and Vernal Umbrella; Inkwell Fan — Moon Shatter Spring; and Anxi Army Assist plus its Trigger Skills for Snowparting Blade and Phalanxbane Blade.

Short Description

Cleftpeak rewards precise deflection and constant boss/player aggression with a short but powerful damage amp. It is especially strong for blade, umbrella, fan and Anxi Army assist setups.

How to Obtain

Sword Trials, Hero's Realm, Outposts, Co-op rewards, and the Dasan Pass boss route.

Ideal Usage

Excels in parry-heavy and assist-oriented builds that frequently Deflect or stay on top of bosses. Reliable Deflects instantly give the full five-stack payoff, making Cleftpeak particularly effective in PvE boss encounters. Defensive pairings such as Agile Steps or Eaglerise help maintain aggression and Deflect uptime.
