Defensive Armor Set

Honorbound Set

A Perfect-Dodge defensive set for players who prefer evasive timing over parry-focused defense. Successful Perfect Dodges grant a short burst of HP, Critical and Affinity damage mitigation.
Set Type: Defensive Armor Set
Equips in the Head, Chest, Bracers and Greaves slots.

Set Bonuses

2 PiecesPhysical Defense
4 PiecesTriggering a Perfect Dodge grants Honorbound for 5 seconds. Honorbound reduces HP Damage taken by 10% and further reduces Critical DMG and Affinity DMG taken by 12%. Honorbound can only be gained once every 10 seconds.

Short Description

Honorbound rewards clean evasive play. It is designed for players who would rather dodge incoming attacks than risk repeated parries, turning a successful Perfect Dodge into a strong temporary defensive window.

Because the buff has a 10-second trigger cooldown and lasts 5 seconds, consistent Perfect Dodge timing is important if you want to keep its protection available during dangerous enemy strings.

Commonly Used Weapons

All weapons can benefit from Honorbound. It has especially strong synergy with fast, evasive weapon styles such as:

Heavenwill GauntletsSkygrasp Rope DartInfernal Twinblades
Weapon recommendations are optional. You do not need to follow these pairings for the set bonus to function.

How to Obtain

Honorbound pieces can be obtained through Hero Trials and Campaign gear boxes that contain the Honorbound set. Set-selectable gear rewards and gear conversion can also be used when available.

Ideal Usage

Honorbound is best for dodge-reliant mobile DPS and defensive hybrid builds. It works especially well for players using Infernal Twinblades, Gauntlets or Rope Dart styles that naturally reposition often and can consistently trigger Perfect Dodges.

The set provides no special reward for standing still and blocking; its value comes from actively avoiding attacks and converting good dodge timing into a five-second defensive advantage.

Best fit: evasive melee, mobile DPS, Perfect-Dodge specialists, PvP counterplay and boss encounters where dodging is safer than committing to parries.
