Swaying Heights Set

Aggressive opener set that deals more damage while the target is still healthy.
Set Type: Offensive Weapon & Accessory Set
Equips in your weapon slots, Disc and Pendant.

Set Bonuses

2 PiecesMin Physical Attack +1
4 PiecesIncreases damage dealt against targets above 50% HP by 5%. Every additional 5% HP the target has further increases this bonus by 1%, up to a maximum total bonus of 10%.

Short Description

Swaying Heights rewards aggressive opening pressure against full-health bosses, elites and mobs while the Min Physical Attack bonus helps keep your damage floor consistent.

Commonly Used Weapons

Infernal Twinblades + Mortal Rope DartStrategic Sword + Heavenquaker SpearInfernal Twinblades (Main) + Strategic SwordNameless Sword + Heavenquaker Spear
Weapon recommendations are optional.

How to Obtain

No notable campaign boss is required. Focus on Hero Trials and Outposts for this set.

Ideal Usage

Best for opener-heavy PvE builds that want to burn through the first half of an enemy health bar quickly. Fast weapons and consistent rotations benefit most because they can capitalize on the high-HP damage window immediately.
