Formbend Set

A Qi-management and shield-focused defensive set that extends shield uptime and heavily reduces HP damage while your Qi remains high or a Qi-damage immunity shield is active.
Set Type: Defensive Armor Set
Equips in the Helm, Chest Armor, Bracers and Greaves slots.

Set Bonuses

2 PiecesPhysical Defense +1
4 PiecesYour shield duration is extended by 2 seconds. If you have more than 85% Qi or a Qi damage immunity shield, all HP damage taken is reduced by 20%.

Short Description

Formbend turns shield-based rotations into a durable frontline playstyle. The extra shield duration makes short defensive windows easier to maintain, while the 20% HP damage reduction rewards keeping Qi above 85% or repeatedly generating Qi-protection effects.

It works especially well with Daunting Strike loops and Nameless Sword / Nameless Spear setups that can keep Qi shields active, allowing you to stay aggressive without giving up tankiness.

Commonly Used Weapons

Ideal Usage

Formbend excels in tank and sustain builds where Qi management and shield uptime are already part of the normal rotation. Keeping Qi above the 85% threshold lets the set provide consistent HP damage reduction, while shield extensions give you more room to attack, reposition, or protect teammates before your defensive window expires.

It is particularly strong in PvE co-op, dungeons and boss fights where predictable incoming damage lets you plan around shield uptime instead of relying on emergency recovery after taking a hit.

Best fit: tanks, bruisers, shield-loop builds, high-Qi setups, and players using Nameless Sword / Spear or Stonesplit-style frontline weapons.

Recommended Pairing

Jadeware complements Formbend by adding offensive pressure while Formbend handles the defensive side of the build. This is especially effective for durable tank/sub-DPS setups that want to stay protected without sacrificing burst damage.
