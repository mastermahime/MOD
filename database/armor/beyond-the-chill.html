Beyond the Chill Set

A defensive set built around avoiding damage long enough to trigger a powerful short-duration protection window. It works best for cautious ranged and control-focused playstyles that can maintain distance before absorbing an incoming burst.
Set Type: Defensive Armor Set
Equips in the Helm, Chest Armor, Bracers and Greaves slots.

Set Bonuses

2 PiecesMax HP +40
4 PiecesAfter entering combat, if no damage is taken for 10 seconds, gain Beyond the Chill. This reduces the next damage taken and all damage received during the following 2 seconds by 40%. The effect is removed upon leaving combat.

Short Description

Beyond the Chill rewards safe ranged poke and controlled openings. The goal is to stay untouched long enough to trigger the 10-second protection effect, then use that window to safely absorb a burst or continue pressure. It performs especially well with mobile, airborne and immobilize-heavy setups that can create enough space to keep the timer active.

Players using ranged weapon combinations often pair it with Veil of the Willow / Mistwillow for additional combo-focused damage while maintaining distance.

Ideal Usage

This is an early-game defensive option for players who prefer cautious positioning, ranged pressure and controlled engagements. It is strongest when you can reliably avoid chip damage while waiting for the 10-second trigger.

The main drawback is consistency. In fast-paced fights, frequent stray hits can repeatedly reset the timer, which makes the set less reliable than a universal always-on tank option. It performs much better against encounters where you can maintain distance, use immobilization or airborne control, and choose when to re-enter danger.

Best fit: ranged poke, mobile control, cautious boss progression, and builds that can keep high uptime on the 10-second no-damage condition.

Recommended Pairing

Veil of the Willow / Mistwillow is a strong companion set concept for players using Inkwell Fan or Vernal Umbrella openers. Beyond the Chill covers the defensive side of the setup while the ranged weapon package focuses on safe combo damage and control.
