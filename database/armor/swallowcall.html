Swallowcall Set

A fast, light-attack-focused offensive set that becomes especially dangerous once enemy Qi is low or a Qi Anomaly is active.
Set Type: Offensive Weapon & Accessory Set
Equips in your two Weapon slots, Disc and Pendant slots.

Set Bonuses

2 PiecesMin Physical Attack +1
4 PiecesLight Attacks and effects triggered by Light Attacks deal 12% more HP damage. They also deal 6% more Physical and Bamboocut damage to targets below 40% Qi or suffering from a Qi Anomaly, including Bone Corrosion and Qi Imbalance.

Short Description

Swallowcall is built around rapid Light Attacks, Qi pressure and mobility. It rewards players who can force low-Qi windows quickly and then stay aggressive long enough to capitalize on the bonus damage.

It pairs naturally with Calmwaters for a high-mobility setup where Perfect Dodges provide sustain while Swallowcall handles the offensive pressure.

Ideal Usage

Swallowcall suits high-risk, high-reward assassin gameplay built around quick strikes, dodging and aggressively draining enemy Qi. It is especially effective in PvP and fast-paced PvE where you can repeatedly push targets below the Qi threshold or apply Qi Anomalies.

Infernal Twinblades and Mortal Rope Dart are standout choices because their mobility and fast attack patterns make it easier to maintain Light Attack pressure and exploit low-Qi targets before they recover.

Best fit: rapid Light Attack rotations, Qi-break pressure, mobile assassin builds, Bamboocut damage and players who can consistently exploit low-Qi windows.

Recommended Pairing

Calmwaters complements Swallowcall by rewarding Perfect Dodges with HP and Endurance sustain, helping aggressive mobile builds stay alive while they chase low-Qi finish windows.
