Tiltrim

An offensive set built around Martial Art Special Enhancement windows and repeated damage.
2-PieceIncreases Physical Attack.
4-PieceWhile Martial Art Special Enhancement is active, dealing damage builds short-duration attack stacks up to five. At maximum stacks, an Inebriate-enhanced skill gains an additional HP-damage payoff.
Best FitBamboocut - Draught / aggressive Special Enhancement builds.

Ideal Usage

Tiltrim is strongest when your rotation can keep landing hits throughout the enhancement window. The Draught pair naturally fits the condition because its drunken-state loop rewards committing to an empowered sequence rather than isolated pokes.

Commonly Used Weapons

Skystrike Gauntlets and Riven Twinblades.
