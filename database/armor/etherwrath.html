Etherwrath Set

A stacking offensive set built around sustained boss/player pressure, Martial Trigger Effects and Perfect Dodge timing. It is the signature offensive pairing for Bamboocut — Kite Gauntlet setups.
Set Type: Offensive Weapon & Accessory Set
Equips in your weapon slots, Disc and Pendant.

Set Bonuses

2 PiecesMin Physical Attack +78
4 PiecesHitting a boss or player grants Etherwrath for 8 seconds, increasing all Attack types by 1.2% per stack, up to 5 stacks. At 5 stacks, applicable Martial Trigger Effects gain +6 Attribute Attack Penetration for all types. Triggering a Perfect Dodge immediately grants 5 stacks.

Enhanced Skills

The full-stack penetration bonus applies to supported Martial Trigger Effects, including Heavenwill Gauntlets — Falcon's Pursuit and the Vile Condemned series, Mortal Rope Dart — Rodent, Everspring Umbrella — Resonance, and Phalanxbane Blade — Anxi Army Assist.

Short Description

Etherwrath rewards staying active against bosses and players. Repeated hits build a short-duration stacking Attack buff, while a successful Perfect Dodge skips the ramp and immediately activates all five stacks.

At maximum stacks, supported Martial Trigger Effects also gain extra Attribute Attack Penetration, making the set especially valuable for builds whose damage loop repeatedly triggers those effects.

Commonly Used Weapons

Weapon recommendations are optional. Etherwrath is most valuable when your build can maintain stacks and repeatedly activate one of its supported Martial Trigger Effects.

How to Obtain

Etherwrath pieces are associated with the Mirkvale and Skyward City Ruins campaign routes. They can also appear through Sword Trials, Hero's Realm, random Outpost rewards, and selectable seasonal gear chests when Etherwrath is offered.

Ideal Usage

Etherwrath is strongest in sustained boss fights, PvP and aggressive PvE where you can keep hitting the target often enough to refresh the eight-second buff. Bamboocut — Kite benefits heavily because Heavenwill Gauntlets can repeatedly exploit the full-stack trigger bonus, while Perfect Dodge gives the build an immediate five-stack burst window.

It also works as an offensive option for other supported trigger-effect builds, particularly those using Mortal Rope Dart, Everspring Umbrella or Phalanxbane Blade.

Best fit: Bamboocut — Kite, Heavenwill Gauntlets, Martial Trigger Effect builds, Perfect-Dodge players, sustained boss DPS and PvP pressure.
