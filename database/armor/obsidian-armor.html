Obsidian Armor Set

A Tenacity-focused defensive set for parry, counter and heavy-trading builds. It rewards maintaining Tenacity by reducing direct HP damage and stacking resistance against Critical and Affinity damage.
Set Type: Defensive Armor Set
Equips in the Helmet, Chest Armor, Bracers and Greaves slots.

Set Bonuses

2 PiecesPhysical Defense Increase
4 PiecesTenacity reduces HP damage taken by 5%. Taking a hit while under Tenacity grants Obsidian Armor for 5 seconds, reducing the chance to receive Critical DMG by 2% and Affinity DMG by 1%. This effect stacks up to 5 times.

Short Description

Obsidian Armor, also referred to as Ebonward in some references, is built for parry/counter-heavy and Tenacity-focused defensive setups. The set becomes stronger the more consistently your build can enter or maintain Tenacity during close-range exchanges.

At full stacks, the set provides strong protection against burst-focused opponents by lowering the chance of taking Critical and Affinity damage while you continue fighting aggressively.

Ideal Usage

This set excels in PvE boss fights and PvP situations where you can reliably trigger Tenacity. The immediate 5% HP damage reduction gives you a solid baseline while the stacking Critical and Affinity resistance makes repeated trades safer against burst-heavy enemies.

It is especially effective for players who prefer to stay aggressive while defending through parries, counters and Tenacity rather than relying on passive shielding or long-distance avoidance.

Best fit: Tenacity uptime, parry/counter builds, Snowparting Blade, Phalanxbane Blade, PvP duels and boss encounters with predictable heavy hits.
