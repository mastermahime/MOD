Obsidian Armor Set

Tenacity-driven mitigation set paired naturally with Stonesplit - Strength.
2-Piece ThemePhysical Defense
4-PieceTenacity reduces HP damage taken. Being hit while under Tenacity builds short-duration Obsidian Armor stacks that lower the chance of receiving Critical and Affinity damage.
SourcesSeasonal gear chests, Sword Trials and Liangzhou campaign/endgame rewards.

Ideal Use

Strong for Snowparting Blade / Phalanxbane Blade and other close-range builds that deliberately maintain Tenacity while trading into enemies.
