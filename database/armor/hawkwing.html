Hawkwing Set

Affinity proc set that ramps Physical Attack during sustained offense.
2-Piece ThemeAffinity Rate
4-PieceWhenever your damage triggers Affinity, gain a short Physical Attack increase that can stack up to five times.
Recommended StyleFrequent-hit and bleed-oriented builds, including Strategic Sword / Heavenquaker Spear.

Ideal Use

Use Hawkwing when your build produces enough hits to keep the Affinity-driven attack stacks active through most of the fight.
