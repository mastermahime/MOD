Hawkwing Set

An Affinity-driven offensive set that rewards frequent procs with stacking Physical Attack, making it especially effective for fast, sustained damage builds.
Set Type: Offensive Weapon & Accessory Set
Equips in your two Weapon slots, Disc and Pendant slots.

Set Bonuses

2 PiecesAffinity Rate +0.1%
4 PiecesWhenever any of your damage triggers Affinity, gain Hawkwing for 5 seconds. Each stack increases Physical Attack by 2%, stacking up to 5 times for a maximum of 10% Physical Attack.

Short Description

Hawkwing is built for fast-paced, burst-heavy and sustained DPS setups that can trigger Affinity repeatedly. The faster you generate Affinity procs, the easier it is to build and refresh its Physical Attack stacks.

It pairs especially well with Eaglerise, giving a balanced offense/defense setup for longer encounters: Hawkwing handles the damage ramp while Eaglerise provides defensive value from DoT or healing uptime.

Ideal Usage

Hawkwing excels in solo DPS and PvE encounters where your build can trigger Affinity quickly and keep attacking long enough to maintain multiple stacks. At full stacks, the set provides a strong Physical Attack ramp without forcing a defensive or low-HP condition.

Strategic Sword and Heavenquaker Spear are natural fits because sustained pressure gives the set repeated chances to trigger Affinity. Nameless Sword and Nameless Spear can also use Hawkwing effectively when built around frequent hits and reliable Affinity generation.

Best fit: Affinity-focused DPS, rapid-hit rotations, sustained boss damage, and players who want offensive scaling that becomes stronger as a fight continues.

Recommended Pairing

Eaglerise is a strong defensive companion. Together, the two sets create a balanced sustained-fight package: Hawkwing ramps Physical Attack while Eaglerise rewards DoT/healing uptime with damage reduction and Eagle Guard.
