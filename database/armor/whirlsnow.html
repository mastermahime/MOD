Whirlsnow Set

Emergency-healing set designed to recover from a massive hit or critical-low-HP state.
2-Piece ThemePhysical Defense
4-PieceAfter losing a large portion of Max HP in one hit or dropping very low, the next healing received shortly afterward restores a large additional amount of Max HP.
SourcesSeasonal gear chests and Sword Trials.

Ideal Use

Good insurance for healers and PvE players learning dangerous encounters, though its long trigger cooldown makes it less consistent than always-on mitigation.
