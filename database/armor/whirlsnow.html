Whirlsnow Set

A clutch sustain set designed to turn heavy burst damage or critical-low HP into a large follow-up heal. It is especially useful for healer, support and sub-DPS builds that can reliably receive healing immediately after a dangerous hit.
Set Type: Defensive Armor Set
Equips in the Helm, Chest Armor, Bracers and Greaves slots.

Set Bonuses

2 PiecesPhysical Defense +1
4 PiecesWhen you lose more than 40% of Max HP in a single instance of damage, or when your HP falls below 20%, the next healing you receive within 5 seconds restores an additional 25% of your Max HP. This effect can trigger once every 60 seconds.

Short Description

Whirlsnow is easy to trigger during burst-heavy encounters or whenever your HP suddenly drops into the danger zone. Its 4-piece effect gives the next heal a massive Max-HP-based boost, making it excellent for clutch recovery and emergency self-sustain.

The set is most valuable when paired with reliable healing tools so the 5-second follow-up window is not wasted.

Ideal Usage

Whirlsnow excels in support and sustain builds that expect to stand near dangerous burst damage while maintaining access to healing. The set rewards aggressive positioning because a large incoming hit can immediately set up a powerful recovery spike.

It is particularly useful in PvE co-op, dungeons and boss fights where healers and support players can consistently trigger the bonus with Panacea Fan, Soulshade Umbrella or allied healing. The long 60-second cooldown means it should be treated as a clutch safety net rather than constant passive defense.

Best fit: healer, support and hybrid sub-DPS builds that can guarantee a heal inside the 5-second trigger window.

Recommended Pairing

Ivorybloom is a natural offensive companion for Whirlsnow. Ivorybloom supports healer and crit-healing playstyles, while Whirlsnow adds a large emergency recovery effect when you or your group takes heavy burst damage.

Panacea Fan and Soulshade Umbrella are especially comfortable choices because their healing-focused kits make it much easier to capitalize on Whirlsnow's 4-piece proc before the 5-second window expires.
