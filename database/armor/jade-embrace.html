Jadeclasp Set

A mobility-triggered defensive set that rewards rapid repositioning with temporary HP and Qi damage reduction. It has particularly natural synergy with Bamboocut - Dust and other Martial Skill-heavy rotations.
Set Type: Defensive Armor Set
Equips in the Helm, Chest Armor, Bracers and Greaves slots.

Set Bonuses

2 PiecesMax HP +40
4 PiecesWhen moving more than 6 meters within 1 second, gain Jadeclasp for 5 seconds. Jadeclasp reduces HP and Qi damage taken by 15%. This effect can only trigger once every 20 seconds.

Short Description

Jadeclasp is built around fast movement and aggressive repositioning. Any rotation that can quickly cover the 6-meter trigger distance can activate a five-second defensive window, making the set especially effective for mobile Martial Art-heavy play.

Bamboocut - Dust has particularly strong natural synergy because Everspring Umbrella and Unfettered Rope Dart builds frequently reposition during their normal damage loop.

Commonly Used Weapons

Everspring UmbrellaUnfettered Rope Dart

These two Martial Arts form the Bamboocut - Dust weapon path and naturally benefit from Jadeclasp's movement condition.

Weapon recommendations are optional. Other Martial Skills, Infernal-style mobility tools, dashes and rapid movement effects can also trigger the set when they cover enough distance.

Ideal Usage

Jadeclasp is strongest in builds that move aggressively as part of their normal rotation rather than standing in place. Martial Skill-heavy setups, fast dashes and abilities that quickly reposition you can trigger the 15% HP and Qi damage reduction without forcing you to interrupt your damage sequence.

It is especially useful in longer boss encounters where Bamboocut - Dust can repeatedly reposition, deal sustained damage and use the movement proc as a defensive layer during dangerous attack windows.

Best fit: Bamboocut - Dust, Everspring Umbrella, Unfettered Rope Dart, Martial Skill-heavy rotations and other high-mobility builds capable of covering 6 meters in under a second.
