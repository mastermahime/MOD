Eaglerise Set

A sustained mitigation set that rewards damage-over-time pressure and healing uptime. Build Eaglerise stacks during normal combat to gain steady damage reduction, then trigger Eagle Guard for protection against a major incoming hit.
Set Type: Defensive Armor Set
Equips in the Helm, Chest Armor, Bracers and Greaves slots.

Set Bonuses

2 PiecesPhysical Defense +1
4 PiecesDealing damage over time or healing grants 1 stack of Eaglerise, reducing damage taken by 1.2% for 10 seconds. This stacks up to 5 times for a total of 6% damage reduction. At maximum stacks, gain Eagle Guard, which reduces the next incoming damage within 10 seconds by 90%. The reduction is halved when the incoming damage is from a boss. Eagle Guard can trigger once every 30 seconds.

Short Description

Eaglerise turns consistent DoT or healing activity into reliable defensive value. Maintaining five stacks gives 6% damage reduction, while Eagle Guard provides a powerful safety layer against the next large hit.

Because both damage-over-time and healing can maintain the effect, the set fits naturally into bleed, poison, hybrid sustain and healer-focused setups.

Ideal Usage

Eaglerise excels in sustain and defensive builds where your normal rotation already contains frequent DoT ticks or healing. Instead of requiring a special defensive action, the set rewards keeping your offensive or support engine active.

At full stacks, the steady 6% damage reduction improves survivability throughout the fight. Eagle Guard then adds a large one-hit protection effect every 30 seconds, making the set particularly valuable in encounters with predictable burst damage.

Against bosses, Eagle Guard's protection is reduced, so the set should be viewed as strong layered mitigation rather than immunity to boss mechanics.

Best fit: bleed/DoT builds, healers, hybrid sustain setups, and players who want passive defense generated through normal combat uptime.

Recommended Pairings

Strategic Sword + Heavenquaker Spear is a natural pairing because sustained pressure and damage-over-time effects help maintain Eaglerise stacks. Healing-focused combinations such as Infernal Twinblades + Panacea Fan can also keep stacks active while providing recovery and support.
