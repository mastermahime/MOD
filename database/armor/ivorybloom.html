Ivorybloom Set

Hybrid support/DPS set that rewards staying at Max HP with stronger Critical damage and healing.
Set Type: Offensive Weapon & Accessory Set
Equips in your weapon slots, Disc and Pendant.

Set Bonuses

2 PiecesCritical Rate +0.1%
4 PiecesAt Max HP, gain a bonus 5% chance to deal Critical healing and damage, and increase the effects of Critical healing and damage by 15.

Short Description

Ivorybloom is a hybrid support/DPS set built around maintaining full HP. It trades some raw always-on damage for stronger critical healing and burst windows, making it especially useful for ranged healers and support damage builds.

Ideal Usage

Best for sustain and support roles in boss fights or co-op where you can reliably stay near full HP. Panacea Fan and Soulshade Umbrella naturally complement the healing side of the set, while the Critical bonuses allow the same build to contribute meaningful burst damage.
