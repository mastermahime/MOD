Ivorybloom Set

Critical-focused set that rewards staying at full HP.
2-Piece ThemeCritical Rate
4-PieceAt Max HP, adds an extra chance for Critical damage/healing and improves the effect of those criticals.
SourcesSeasonal gear chests, Sword Trials and campaign drops such as Zheng the Frostwing.

Ideal Use

Strong for ranged/healing builds that can reliably remain at full HP; much less consistent in PvP or attrition-heavy fights.
