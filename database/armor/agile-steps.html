Agile Steps Set

A high-skill defensive set that rewards successful deflections with short, stackable bursts of heavy damage reduction.
Set Type: Defensive Armor Set
Equips in the Helm, Chest Armor, Bracers and Greaves slots.

Set Bonuses

2 PiecesMax HP +40
4 PiecesUpon a successful deflection, gain the Agile Steps effect: reduces all damage taken by 20% for 2 seconds, stacking up to 3 times for a maximum of 60% damage reduction.

Short Description

Agile Steps turns accurate deflection timing into powerful burst mitigation. Players who can chain successful deflections can build up to 60% damage reduction, making the set especially strong for parry-focused tanking and counterplay.

Ideal Usage

Agile Steps is ideal for players who reliably deflect enemy attacks. It shines in high-skill PvE boss encounters, dungeons and PvP where precise timing lets you repeatedly trigger the two-second reduction window and stack it for very high temporary mitigation.

The set offers less value when deflections are inconsistent, but in experienced hands it can provide much stronger burst protection than passive defensive sets.

Recommended Pairing

Pairing Agile Steps with the Hawkwing offensive set and weapons such as Nameless Sword or Strategic Sword creates a counter-focused setup built around perfect blocks, deflections and immediate retaliation.

Best fit: deflection masters, parry tanks, counterattack builds, PvP duels and boss encounters with predictable attack strings.
