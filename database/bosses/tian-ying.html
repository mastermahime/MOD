Tian Ying

Campaign boss from The Promised Light, with multiple Mystic Skill connections.

Unlock

Complete The Promised Light campaign questline.

Related Mystic Skills

Tian Ying is connected to Yaksha Rush and later study/rematch requirements for Free Morph.

Why Revisit

This is one of the campaign encounters worth keeping unlocked and familiar because skill progression can send you back after the story clear.
