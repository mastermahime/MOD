Overview

Wucan is a major western campaign encounter associated with Liangzhou progression and uses a distinctive sorcery-heavy combat presentation.

Preparation

Enter with late-region gear, a coherent Martial Path and enough mobility to respond to ranged or area-pressure mechanics.
