Moongazing Maiden

Later western world boss found around Jade-Mirrored Spring.

Location

Found at Jade-Mirrored Spring in the western Hexi/Liangzhou content.

Combat Theme

The encounter is known for fast twinblade pressure, tail attacks and moonlit lightning effects. Keep enough mobility to disengage from large area threats.
