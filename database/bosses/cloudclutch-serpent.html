Cloudclutch Serpent

Fast mechanical serpent built around constant repositioning, poison and segmented movement.

Location

Found in Hidden Mountain's Suixiang area. Travel toward the easternmost island and interact with the Curious Mechanical Cauldron to begin the encounter.

Combat

Unlike the fortress-like Inkpincer Scorpion, Cloudclutch Serpent constantly changes position. Keep camera control stable, avoid poison zones and save gap-closing tools for moments when the serpent creates distance.

Version 2.1

The serpent and Inkpincer Scorpion were designed as the signature mechanical world bosses of the Hidden Mountain/Mohist Hill update.
