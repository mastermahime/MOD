Puppeteer - Curtaincall

World boss located on a small island along the northwest edge of Sundara Land.

Location

Travel toward the northwest border of Sundara Land and look for the isolated island encounter.

Preparation

Make sure your healing, weapon durability and fast-travel access are ready before committing to repeated attempts.
