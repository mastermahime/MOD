Inkpincer Scorpion

Heavily armored Mohist mechanical boss whose pincer shields must be broken before its Qi becomes vulnerable.

Location

In Hidden Mountain's Suixiang area, south of Heaven's Threshold. Enter the cave on the east side of the mountain road and interact with the mechanical cauldron to reach the Hero's Tomb arena.

Mechanics

  • Attack from behind to exploit the Mechanism Weakness and break pincer shields faster.
  • Deflect the heavy/red-glint slam sequence for a major shield punish.
  • Bursting Nine can answer the poison-spray mechanic.

Rewards

First-clear and difficulty rewards include regional exploration progress, currencies, EXP and Inner Way-related chests/materials.
