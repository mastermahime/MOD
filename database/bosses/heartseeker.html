Campaign Boss

Heartseeker

Campaign boss unlocked through the Still Shore questline.

Unlock

Complete the Still Shore campaign questline to unlock the encounter.

Preparation

Bring a stable main Martial Path, healing supplies and enough defense to learn attack timings instead of forcing damage trades.

Repeat Farming

Campaign bosses can be important for progression and Talent objectives. First clears may use different reward rules than later Energy-spending clears.
