Soaring Spin

Single-target control skill hidden in the Martial Temple route.

How to Unlock

Reach the Martial Temple statue chamber and open the hidden chest above the large statue. Celestial Seize makes the chest much easier to interact with.

Use

Provides a control-oriented spinning attack that can complement builds lacking a reliable single-target control Mystic Skill.
