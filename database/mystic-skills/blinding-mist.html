Mystic Skill

Blinding Mist

A Moonveil Mountain Jianghu Legacy technique tied to Blind to the World.

How to Unlock

Progress Blind to the World / Jianghu Legacy 04 in Moonveil Mountain and complete the required Moonlit Flower offering.

Tip

If the final interaction is unavailable, check that you have the Moonlit Flower and are on the correct step of the Legacy quest.
