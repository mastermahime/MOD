Hexi Offensive Mystic Skill

Flute of the Tides

Wide-area damage skill unlocked through a music-focused Hexi exploration quest.

How to Unlock

  1. Reach Peaceward Camp in Jade Gate Pass, Hexi.
  2. Play Kalavinka for Monk Unknowing to trigger the quest.
  3. Perform at the marked location in Whitecrown Fortress.
  4. Perform again at Heroic Tomb / Heroes' Grave.
  5. Return to Monk Unknowing to receive the skill.

Effect

Unleashes a broad damage burst, then summons a flute attack that continues striking nearby enemies.
