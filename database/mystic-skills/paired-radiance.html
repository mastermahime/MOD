Paired Radiance

Exclusive Partnership Mystic Skill for coordinated co-op play.

How to Unlock

Reach Partnership Level 3.

Use

When teamed with your partner, the skill performs a paired cooperative movement/teleport effect and can be used in supported co-op content such as Hero's Realm, Sword Trial and co-op rooms.
