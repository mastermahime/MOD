Ghostly Steps

Dodge-support skill that grants Mirage and reduces ground-dodge Endurance use.

How to Unlock

Complete the Cloudwalk Spire Divinecraft route east of Ghostlight Market and open the final chest.

Effect

Mirage lasts for a short window, reducing ground-dodge Endurance cost and extending the period in which you are difficult to target.
