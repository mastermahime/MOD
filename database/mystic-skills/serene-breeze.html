Mystic Skill

Serene Breeze

A talent-progression Mystic Skill rather than an exploration or Skill Theft unlock.

How to Unlock

Invest through the required route in the Basic Talent tree. The current unlock route requires approximately 12 points along that path.

Tip

Plan the point path before spending if Serene Breeze is your immediate goal; unrelated talent spending may not advance the required branch.
