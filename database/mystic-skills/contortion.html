Contortion

Shrinks your character for miniature exploration spaces and special event interactions.

How to Unlock

Complete Tiny Adventure: Part 1 from Elder Zhan at Riverside Station in Qinghe. The quest requires early-game progression and unlocks the Contortion skill before the Miniature Treasure route.

Use

Equip Contortion when a puzzle, miniature chest or narrow exploration space specifically calls for the smaller form.
