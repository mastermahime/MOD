Mystic Skill

Glow of Fireflies

An exploration skill learned by collecting fireflies around Crimson Cliffs Lake.

How to Unlock

Collect five fireflies around Crimson Cliffs Lake, especially near the glowing-flower areas where they are easiest to spot.

Tip

Search the lake perimeter rather than one single spawn. The unlock tracks multiple collection interactions.
