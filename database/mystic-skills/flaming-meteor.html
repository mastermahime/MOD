Mystic Skill

Flaming Meteor

A study-based combat skill learned during the Bodhi Sea / Ye Wanshan challenge route.

How to Unlock

Progress the Bodhi Sea challenge and study Ye Wanshan's signature plunge. Fill the required study/proficiency meter and complete the encounter successfully.

Tip

The study step matters: simply defeating the target without completing the observation/proficiency requirement may not award the skill.
