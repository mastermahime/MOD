Bursting Nine

Hidden Mountain archery Mystic Skill that unleashes a nine-arrow formation and heavy area pressure.

How to Unlock

Finish Mountains Unveiled, reach Hidden Mountain, and complete the Evershift Labyrinth Jianghu Legacy.

Effect

Fires a rapid formation of Bronze Arrows that rains projectiles into an area, dealing strong damage and applying heavy posture/Endurance pressure.

Boss Utility

Bursting Nine is especially useful against the Inkpincer Scorpion, where the mechanic prompt can use the arrow formation to answer its poison attack.
