Mystic Skill

Tai Chi

A signature control-oriented Mystic Skill learned through an early Qinghe encounter.

How to Unlock

Complete A Bear of a Task in Verdant Wilds. Follow the encounter prompts and complete the Tai Chi interaction to learn the skill.

Usage

Tai Chi is valuable when an encounter allows its contextual control interaction, giving you a way to redirect or manipulate enemies and objects rather than relying only on weapon damage.
