Free Morph

A study-based skill tied to a qualifying Tian Ying rematch.

How to Unlock

Reach the eligible Halo Peak Tian Ying phase-two rematch and complete the study/observation requirement during the fight.

Tip

The correct rematch phase matters. If the study prompt is missing, verify that you are fighting the eligible version rather than an earlier encounter.
