Mystic Skill

Meridian Touch

A pressure-point technique obtained through the Yan Qiren / Stonewash Strand progression route.

How to Unlock

Progress the Yan Qiren sequence around Stonewash Strand and the related Jianghu Legacy 03 route.

Usage

Meridian Touch is primarily a control tool. Use it when disabling or interrupting a target creates more value than another raw-damage skill.
