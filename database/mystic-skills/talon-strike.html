Mystic Skill

Talon Strike

An observation skill learned from a bird near Palace of Annals.

How to Unlock

Search the lower western cliff south of Palace of Annals for the relevant bird nest, then use the bird observation/study interaction.

Tip

The route is below the more obvious palace elevation; approach the western cliff at a lower height if you cannot locate the nest.
