Mystic Skill

Dragon's Breath

A Wandering Tale reward associated with Beast Reverie and the Fairgrounds.

How to Unlock

Complete the relevant Beast Reverie / Fairgrounds Wandering Tale steps, use the required Celestial Seize interaction/item when prompted, then claim the Wandering Tales reward.

Tip

Remember to claim the completed tale reward; finishing the activity without claiming its reward can make the skill appear to be missing.
