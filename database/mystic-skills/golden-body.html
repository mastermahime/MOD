Mystic Skill

Golden Body

A defensive Mystic Skill learned through a timed Skill Theft route at the Grand Imperial Temple.

Prerequisite

Learn Celestial Seize first.

How to Unlock

Go to the Grand Imperial Temple and complete the Skill Theft interaction involving Shi Yi at the appropriate time/state.

Tip

If the theft prompt is unavailable, check both the Celestial Seize prerequisite and the timing/quest state before retrying the location.
