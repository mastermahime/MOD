Goosy Slide

Comedic campaign skill with a chance to instantly clear eligible campaign bosses.

How to Unlock

Granted automatically to eligible players after the Version 1.5 As Snow Falls update. It appears in the Mystic Skill menu and does not use the normal breakthrough system.

Effect

In eligible Campaign Challenges, using Goosy Slide has a chance to instantly clear the stage. Some major campaigns are excluded and the effect has a daily-use limit.
