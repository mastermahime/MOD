Mystic Skill

Wolflike Frenzy

A direct exploration pickup found above the Ghostlight Market entrance.

How to Unlock

Reach the upper ledge above the Ghostlight Market entrance and open the lit chest containing the skill.

Tip

Search vertically. The chest is above the entrance area, so staying at street level can make the marker/location easy to miss.
