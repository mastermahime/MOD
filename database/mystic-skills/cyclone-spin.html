Cyclone Spin

Exclusive Discipleship Mystic Skill performed by a Master and their disciples.

How to Unlock

Reach Discipleship Level 4 as the Master. The skill was added to the global game in the August 20, 2026 update.

Use

When teamed with disciples, Cyclone Spin lets the group perform the cooperative spinning technique and also contributes Mentorship Points.
