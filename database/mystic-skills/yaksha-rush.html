Mystic Skill

Yaksha Rush

A combat Mystic Skill tied to The Promised Light and Tian Ying progression.

How to Unlock

Progress The Promised Light story route involving Tian Ying. The skill is awarded through that progression rather than as a generic open-world pickup.

Tip

If you are following a location-only guide and cannot find it, check your Promised Light story progress first.
