Mystic Skill

Dragon Head

A collection milestone reward for players who have learned a large number of Mystic Skills.

How to Unlock

Learn 35 other distinct Mystic Skills. Dragon Head is awarded as a collection/progression milestone rather than from one map location.

Tip

If you are close to the requirement, prioritize simple observation, chest and quest Mystic Skills to reach the count efficiently.
