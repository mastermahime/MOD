Mystic Skill

Touch of Death

A contextual assassination technique earned through the Battlecrest Slope legacy route.

How to Unlock

Progress Echoes of Old Battles / Warrior's Pledge around Battlecrest Slope and complete the contextual assassination interaction required by the quest.

Tip

Follow the quest's stealth/context prompt; defeating the target normally may bypass the interaction you need for the skill.
