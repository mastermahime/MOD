Mystic Skill

Celestial Seize

A remote-grab utility skill that also serves as a prerequisite for some later interactions.

How to Unlock

Complete the Foul Play route around Heaven's Pier.

Why It Matters

Celestial Seize is useful both on its own and as a prerequisite. For example, the Golden Body theft route expects you to already have it.
