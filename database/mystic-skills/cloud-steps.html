Mystic Skill

Cloud Steps

A movement technique found beneath General's Shrine during Echoes in the Shrine.

How to Unlock

Progress Echoes in the Shrine below General's Shrine. Search the base of the long staircase for the corpse/interactable associated with the skill.

Tip

Look below the shrine rather than on the surface grounds; the vertical route is the main source of confusion.
