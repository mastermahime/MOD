World to Sword

Converts surplus healing into an offensive Qi Edge.

How to Unlock

Complete the Raven's Testament Wandering Tale in Heaven's Threshold. In Wu Zhi's cave, read the inscription, use a healing Martial Art beside the Ancient Bronze Sword, pull the sword free, and give it to Wu Zhi.

Effect

Stores excess healing and converts the surplus into a sword/Qi Edge attack. Breakthroughs extend the interaction to more party healing.

Best Use

Excellent for Panacea Fan and Soulshade Umbrella builds that frequently overheal in group content.
