Stonesplit - Might

Rock Solid

Taunt-focused mitigation passive for Storm Roar.

Effect

Taunting increases Storm Roar's damage reduction, trading some outgoing damage for better frontline survival.

How to Obtain

Complete the Unbound Cavern campaign and defeat Dao Lord.

Build Use

Designed for tanking and co-op frontline roles where survival and aggro control matter more than personal DPS.
