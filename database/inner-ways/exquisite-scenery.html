Stonesplit - Might

Exquisite Scenery

Defense-triggered riposte passive with additional boss utility.

Effect

Successful defense can trigger a Defensive Riposte and builds additional combat value against Online Bosses through later tiers.

How to Obtain

Follow its Inner Way Clue/Tips route in the Develop menu; the source is tied to Stonesplit defensive progression.

Build Use

Best on defensive builds that intentionally block/deflect rather than relying only on dodge.
