How Inner Ways Fit a Build

Start with the Martial Path or role you want to support, then pick Inner Ways whose trigger conditions happen naturally in that rotation. A strong passive that rarely activates in your actual play pattern is usually less useful than a smaller bonus you can maintain consistently.
