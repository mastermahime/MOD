General

Seasonal Edge

Dual-Weapon Skill passive that grants one of several temporary effects.

Effect

Casting a Dual-Weapon Skill grants one of several random beneficial effects; higher breakthroughs improve the pool and resource interaction.

How to Obtain

Purchase the ten required pages from Cao Sheng for a total of 5,000 Echo Jade.

Build Use

Useful for builds that actively rotate both weapon slots and trigger Dual-Weapon Skills often.
