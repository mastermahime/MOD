Vendetta

Mortal Rope Dart passive that extends Vendetta Token value and restores Tokens of Gratitude.

Effect

Vendetta Token lasts longer and restores Tokens of Gratitude used by the path's summon/rat mechanics.

How to Obtain

Complete the Jinming Pool: Petalfall Banquet campaign in Kaifeng; the chain culminates in the Murong Yuan encounter.

Build Use

Strong for Mortal Rope Dart-focused Bamboocut - Wind setups that lean into the token/summon loop.
