Evasive Charge

Perfect-Dodge Endurance refund passive.

Effect

A Perfect Dodge has a chance to refund the Endurance consumed by the dodge.

How to Obtain

Complete the Streetwise Sage Jianghu Legacy.

Build Use

Strong for dodge-heavy PvP and mobile ranged/melee setups that frequently spend Endurance on defense.
