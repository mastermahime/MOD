Wildfire Spark

Endurance engine that refunds part of the Endurance you spend.

Effect

Refunds a portion of Endurance consumption, with breakthrough tiers adding stronger recovery and stacking Endurance-recovery effects.

How to Obtain

Complete the Shi Yimo encounter chain beginning after Culinary: Hotpot Fever and Deerforage's Remedy; the final steps involve healing Shi Yimo and delivering wild mushrooms.

Build Use

Very strong for active builds that constantly dodge, sprint or spend Endurance during offense.
