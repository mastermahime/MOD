Skyspeak

A Bamboocut - Draught Inner Way tied to Riven Twinblades and the Inebriate / Deepdaze combat loop.
Best ForRiven Twinblades + Skystrike Gauntlets
RoleDraught state synergy
How to ObtainAfter Echoes of Old Battle, investigate the Skyspeak clue around Battlecrest Slope / Verdant Wilds, talk to Li Yangui, then choose Revisit. The reward includes Skyspeak Tome I and Riven Twinblades.

Recommended Usage

Use Skyspeak as part of the dedicated Draught setup rather than treating it as a universal passive. Its value rises when you deliberately cycle the shared drunken states between the paired weapons.
