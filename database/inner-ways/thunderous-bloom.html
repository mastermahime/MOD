Thunderous Bloom

Spring Thunder passive that increases Heavy and Ballistic HP damage.

Effect

Spring Thunder increases the HP damage dealt by Heavy and Ballistic attacks.

How to Obtain

Complete the Mercyheart Monastery Outpost.

Build Use

Useful for Silkbind - Jade rotations that lean on Heavy and Ballistic attacks rather than only basic ranged pressure.
