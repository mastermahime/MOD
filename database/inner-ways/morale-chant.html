General

Morale Chant

Universal ramping passive for penetration and output.

Effect

Attacking or healing builds Yi River, increasing penetration and damage/output as the effect stacks.

How to Obtain

Purchase the ten required tomes/pages from Tang Bao for a total of 5,000 Echo Jade.

Build Use

Good in sustained PvE and hybrid builds because both attacking and healing can contribute to the passive.
