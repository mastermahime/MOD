Sword Horizon

Strategic Sword timing passive that converts a well-timed follow-up into Crisscrossing Swords.

Effect

Perfect timing after Strategic Sword skills triggers Crisscrossing Swords; with enough Bleed stacks, the follow-up deals significant additional bleed damage.

How to Obtain

Follow the Strategic Sword Inner Way clue / sanctum progression after learning the weapon style.

Build Use

A natural path-specific choice for Strategic Sword + Heavenquaker Spear bleed builds.
