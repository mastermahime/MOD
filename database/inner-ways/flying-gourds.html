Flying Gourds

Inkwell Fan-linked passive that alters Peak's Springless Silence charges and cooldown behavior.

Effect

Grants additional charges to the relevant Inkwell Fan skill while changing its cooldown; higher tiers add extra utility around its repeated use.

How to Obtain

Open the golden chest inside the Inkwell Fan - Sanctum in Sundara Land, Qinghe. The sanctum route uses a Dragon Key.

Build Use

A path-specific option for players building heavily around Inkwell Fan skill cycling.
