Esoteric Revival

Support Inner Way that strengthens Panacea Fan resurrection and post-revive safety.

Effect

Improves resurrection healing and gains stronger post-revive protection through breakthrough tiers.

How to Obtain

Defeat the Sleeping Daoist world boss at Jadebrook Mountain in Qinghe to obtain the tome.

Build Use

A priority Inner Way for dedicated co-op healers using Panacea Fan.
