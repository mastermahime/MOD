Bamboocut - Dust

Light Anew

Multi-target debuff passive that applies Candle Flicker.

Effect

Hitting three or more enemies applies Candle Flicker, slowing movement and increasing damage taken.

How to Obtain

Complete the A Wellspring of Hope Jianghu Legacy.

Build Use

Best in mob-heavy PvE where Bamboocut - Dust can repeatedly tag groups and capitalize on the debuff.
