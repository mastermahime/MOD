General

Bitter Seasons

Poison/debuff passive for sustained damage setups.

Effect

Attacks can apply Poison, dealing damage over time and reducing the target's Physical Defense.

How to Obtain

Find the Bitter Seasons Tome in Gleaming Abyss.

Build Use

Works well in long fights and with builds that benefit from both damage-over-time and defense reduction.
