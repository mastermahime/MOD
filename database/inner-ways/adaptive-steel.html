Adaptive Steel

Weapon-mastery passive whose bonus changes with the active weapon type.

Effect

Grants a weapon-specific mastery bonus based on the weapon you are currently using, making it a flexible option when swapping between different weapon families.

How to Obtain

Trade Oddities with Qi Sheng in Qinghe to obtain the tome.

Build Use

Useful when your loadout changes weapon type often and you want one Inner Way to remain relevant across both slots.
