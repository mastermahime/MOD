Stonesplit - Strength

Frost-Clad Night

Deflect-triggered Stonesplit - Strength passive built around Snowbreak Spring and bonus HP damage.

Effect

Successful deflects and the path's Grave Frost / dual-weapon conditions can trigger a Snowbreak Spring follow-up that adds HP damage and interacts with Stonesplit - Strength's later mechanics.

How to Obtain

Obtained through Liangzhou Stonesplit - Strength progression. Follow the Inner Way clue after unlocking the path in the western region.

Build Use

One of the main path-specific choices for Snowparting Blade + Phalanxbane Blade.
