Shadow Assault

Enhances Touch of Death with better reach, damage and sustain.

Effect

Improves the Touch of Death Mystic Skill, increasing its effective range and damage while adding healing/sustain through breakthrough progression.

How to Obtain

Trade Oddities with Qi Sheng in Qinghe and advance the related reward track.

Build Use

A specialized pick for players who actively use Touch of Death rather than a universal passive.
