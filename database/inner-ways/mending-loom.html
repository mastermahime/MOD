Mending Loom

Soulshade Umbrella sustain passive that restores Dew and healing through Echoing Grow.

Effect

Echoing Grow restores Dew and heals based on Dew consumed, supporting sustained healing loops.

How to Obtain

Progress the Ever-Normal Granary campaign in Kaifeng; the reward appears as the Soul Weave/Mending Loom tome during that chain.

Build Use

A core support option for Soulshade Umbrella and Silkbind - Deluge.
