Mountain's Might

Endurance-efficiency passive for Mountain Sweep and related Bellstrike - Splendor play.

Effect

Mountain Sweep grants Endless Gale, reducing Endurance cost for a short duration.

How to Obtain

Open five chests in the Palace of Annals.

Build Use

Strong for mobile Bellstrike - Splendor players who spend Endurance aggressively during their attack loop.
