Wind Beneath Wings

Airborne mobility passive that reduces dash cost and improves movement after landing.

Effect

Reduces airborne-dash Endurance cost and grants movement-speed value after landing.

How to Obtain

Complete the Echoes of Old Battles/Tiger Fort progression and clear the related Tiger Fort Outpost route.

Build Use

Useful for exploration and highly mobile combat styles that frequently fight or reposition in the air.
