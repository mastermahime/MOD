Song of Tang

Health-state passive that changes its reward depending on whether you are healthy or wounded.

Effect

Martial Arts Skills apply Tang Song. While above 50% HP the effect favors Critical Damage; below 50% HP it shifts toward Life Drain/sustain.

How to Obtain

Complete the In the Quiet of Night Jianghu Legacy.

Build Use

Fits Bamboocut - Dust because the path can reposition to preserve the offensive state or lean on sustain after taking damage.
