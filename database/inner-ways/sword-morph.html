Sword Morph

Nameless Sword charged-attack passive for Vagrant Sword.

Effect

Charging Vagrant Sword unleashes additional sword-energy attacks without adding more Endurance cost beyond the later charge stage.

How to Obtain

Claim the Sword Morph Tome from the consecutive-login reward route (three consecutive days on the tracked global progression).

Build Use

One of the most direct Bellstrike - Splendor damage Inner Ways for Nameless Sword users.
