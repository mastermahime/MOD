Soaring High

A Heavenwill Gauntlets payoff passive that converts a full Heaven's Will state into a stronger heavy attack.
Best ForHeavenwill Gauntlets
Core MechanicAt three Heaven's Will bars, Heavy Attack becomes the enhanced Vile Condemned: End finisher and improves burst potential.
How to ObtainComplete the Mountain Unveiled Jianghu Legacy.

Recommended Usage

This is a direct payoff piece for the Kite path. Pair it with effects that help you generate Heaven's Will reliably so the enhanced Heavy Attack becomes a normal part of the rotation rather than a rare event.
