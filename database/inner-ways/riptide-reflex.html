Bamboocut - Wind

Riptide Reflex

Control-hit cooldown reduction for fast Bamboocut rotations.

Effect

Hitting an enemy with a Control Skill reduces Martial Arts Skill cooldown.

How to Obtain

Complete the Veiled Blades Jianghu Legacy.

Build Use

Excellent when your rotation applies Control regularly and you can convert every reduction into another meaningful skill use.
