Celestial Vigor

A dodge-reward passive that builds sustained combat value after successful Perfect Dodges.
Best ForMobile builds that consistently Perfect Dodge
Core MechanicPerfect Dodge grants Unceasing stacks that improve combat performance.
How to ObtainMohist Hill sect shop.

Recommended Usage

Use Celestial Vigor when your defensive plan already relies on evasion. It turns good dodge timing into a continuing offensive and defensive advantage instead of a one-time escape.

Pairing

It is especially comfortable on Bamboocut - Kite and other agile builds, but the trigger is broad enough to work outside that path if you can maintain it.
