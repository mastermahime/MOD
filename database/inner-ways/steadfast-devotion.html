Steadfast Devotion

Adds a powerful third charge stage to Heart Ablaze.

Effect

Heart Ablaze gains an additional high-power charge stage, with later breakthroughs strengthening the path's heavy payoff and Anxi Soldier interactions.

How to Obtain

Received through the Stonesplit - Strength unlock/progression route in Liangzhou; check the path tutorial/mail reward after learning the path.

Build Use

A priority path-specific Inner Way for Snowparting Blade + Phalanxbane Blade.
