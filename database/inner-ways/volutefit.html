Volutefit

A defensive utility Inner Way added with newer Global content.
Primary UseDamage-reduction / utility support
How to ObtainComplete The Promised Light, then loot the relevant chest inside Scripture Hall in Qinghe's Sundara Land.

Recommended Usage

Volutefit is useful when survivability is more important than squeezing out another purely offensive passive. It can be considered for high-pressure encounters or builds whose main damage engine is already supplied by their other Inner Ways.

Acquisition Note

If the chest is not available, verify that The Promised Light progression requirement is complete before assuming the location is wrong.
