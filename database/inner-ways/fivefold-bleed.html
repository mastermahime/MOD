General

Fivefold Bleed

General damage-over-time passive that applies Weeping Blood.

Effect

Attacks can apply Weeping Blood, dealing damage over time.

How to Obtain

Complete the A Faltering Flame Wandering Tale in Kaifeng and claim its reward.

Build Use

Fits sustained offensive builds and can complement other bleed/DoT mechanics.
