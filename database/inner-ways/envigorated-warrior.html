General

Envigorated Warrior

Simple universal damage-and-healing passive.

Effect

Increases general damage and healing output, making it an easy filler Inner Way when a build does not need a more specialized trigger.

How to Obtain

This Inner Way appears through normal Inner Way progression/reward routes; use the in-game Tips/Clue panel for the currently available source on your version.

Build Use

Useful in almost any early or hybrid setup because it does not demand a narrow weapon condition.
