Bamboocut - Wind

Breaking Point

Critical burst passive for enemies whose Qi has been depleted.

Effect

Critical hits on Spirit-Depleted / low-Qi enemies grant Collapse, increasing penetration and Critical Damage for the punish window.

How to Obtain

Defeat the Yi Dao world boss in Qinghe.

Build Use

Best in aggressive builds that break Qi quickly and can immediately convert the break into critical damage.
