Steadfast Stance

Anti-stagger passive for general PvE stability.

Effect

Reduces the chance of being staggered by normal enemies. Bosses and players are not covered by the same protection.

How to Obtain

Explore the cave beneath/around General's Shrine and follow the Inner Way clue to its chest.

Build Use

Convenient for open-world and mob-heavy PvE, but less valuable in bossing or PvP where the anti-stagger exception matters.
