Throat-Piercing Art

Varied-Combo offensive passive that stacks resistance ignore and Critical Damage.

Effect

Varied Combo attacks grant Throat-Pierced, improving direct Physical Resistance ignore and Critical Damage. Successful deflects can accelerate the stack condition.

How to Obtain

Follow the Liangzhou clue through the vulture/Sparrow Sutra Hall outpost-library route.

Build Use

Strong for Stonesplit - Strength because its normal defensive loop feeds directly into the offensive stacks.
