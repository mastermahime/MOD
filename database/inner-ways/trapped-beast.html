General

Trapped Beast

Emergency low-HP shield passive.

Effect

Dropping below the low-HP threshold grants a large Max-HP-based shield, giving you a recovery window before the effect can trigger again.

How to Obtain

Follow the Inner Way clue in the Develop menu; its acquisition is tied to general exploration/progression rather than one Martial Path.

Build Use

Good progression safety and PvP insurance when you expect sudden burst damage.
