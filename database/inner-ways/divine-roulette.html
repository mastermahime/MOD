Divine Roulette

Deflect passive that grants one of several powerful temporary effects.

Effect

Successful defensive timing can trigger a random beneficial effect such as protection or offensive utility.

How to Obtain

Defeat the Void King in Gleaming Abyss.

Build Use

Flexible for players who deflect consistently and do not require a path-specific Inner Way in the slot.
