Echoes of Oblivion

Infernal Twinblades passive built around Sin/Karma pressure and resistance ignore.

Effect

Infernal Twinblades apply Sin and Karma mechanics that let attacks ignore Physical Defense and Bamboocut Resistance.

How to Obtain

Defeat Qianye during the Blissful Retreat campaign line to obtain the tome.

Build Use

A strong path-specific choice for Infernal Twinblades users.
