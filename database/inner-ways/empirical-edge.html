Empirical Edge

An offensive passive that rewards triggered Martial Art effects with penetration-oriented Cognition stacks.
Best ForMartial Arts that repeatedly trigger special effects
Core MechanicTriggered Martial Art effects build Cognition, improving Physical and Attribute Penetration.
How to ObtainMohist Hill sect shop.

Recommended Usage

Empirical Edge is most valuable when your rotation naturally procs its condition often enough to keep Cognition relevant. It is an offensive choice for players who want more value against tougher defenses.
