Stonesplit - Strength

Wildfire Surge

Tenacity passive that adds damage, mitigation and HP drain based on nearby enemies.

Effect

While Tenacity is active, gain offensive and defensive benefits plus HP Drain; the effect scales with the number of nearby enemies.

How to Obtain

Follow the Liangzhou Inner Way clue through its outpost/statue exploration route after unlocking Stonesplit - Strength.

Build Use

Excellent for close-range Stonesplit - Strength play where Tenacity is already part of the core loop.
