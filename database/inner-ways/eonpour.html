Eonpour

A core offensive Inner Way for the new Bamboocut - Draught weapon pair.
Best ForSkystrike Gauntlets + Riven Twinblades
RoleMartial Arts Attack / Draught resource support
How to ObtainAt Millennium Crossing, defeat Jiu Qiansui, speak to him again, then choose Listen to the Story. The same sequence unlocks Skystrike Gauntlets.

Recommended Usage

Eonpour belongs in builds that commit to the Draught drunken-state loop. It is less attractive as a generic filler Inner Way when you are not using the weapons that trigger its intended conditions.

Do not leave after the Jiu Qiansui duel: the follow-up story conversation is the reward step.
