Mistwing

An offensive support Inner Way associated with attack and Physical Penetration scaling.
Primary UseOffensive scaling / Physical Penetration support
How to ObtainComplete Palace of Annals, then loot the chest at Hero's Tomb below the Palace area in Qinghe's Moonveil Mountain.

Recommended Usage

Mistwing is a straightforward consideration when your build benefits from offensive stat support and you need more performance against enemy defenses.

Acquisition Note

The prerequisite quest progression matters; finish Palace of Annals before searching the Hero's Tomb chest route.
