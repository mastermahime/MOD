Bellstrike - Umbra

Wolfchaser's Art

Heavenquaker Spear passive that accelerates Sorrow Without Wine combo generation against Bleeding targets.

Effect

Reduces the combo count needed for Sorrow Without Wine's buffs and adds extra combo-generation chances when hitting targets affected by your Bleed.

How to Obtain

Open the golden chest near the end of the Heavenquaker Spear - Sanctum east of Wildmane Ranch. The entrance uses a Dragon Key.

Build Use

A core Bellstrike - Umbra option for Heavenquaker Spear / Strategic Sword bleed setups.
