Blossom Barrage

Vernal Umbrella passive that strengthens Spring Sorrow and ballistic pressure.

Effect

Spring Sorrow gains extra stacks and applies Combo, improving Ballistic Skill damage and enabling additional petal-style pressure through higher breakthrough tiers.

How to Obtain

Complete the Shadows in Bloom Jianghu Legacy in Kaifeng.

Build Use

A core option for Vernal Umbrella-focused Silkbind - Jade builds.
