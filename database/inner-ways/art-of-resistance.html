Stonesplit - Might

Art of Resistance

Shield-support passive for defensive frontline builds.

Effect

Extends shield duration and improves shield-related value, helping tank builds keep protection active longer.

How to Obtain

Complete the Scammer Victims Encounter.

Build Use

Pairs naturally with Stonesplit - Might and shield-heavy armor sets such as Formbend or Rainwhisper.
