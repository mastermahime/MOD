Phantom Rally

Phantom Umbrella and Resonance passive for Everspring Umbrella.

Effect

Scarlet Spin summons Phantom Umbrellas, while creating or Perfect-Catching a Phantom Umbrella triggers Resonance explosions.

How to Obtain

Defeat Guo Xin in Whitecrown Fortress/Whitecrown City to obtain the relevant tome.

Build Use

One of the defining Bamboocut - Dust Inner Ways and a natural choice for Everspring Umbrella rotations.
