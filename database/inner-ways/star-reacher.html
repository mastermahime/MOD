Silkbind - Jade

Star Reacher

Pursuit/airborne passive tied to Lingering Bone Mark.

Effect

Lingering Bone Mark grants a Physical Attack bonus and gains additional value when you knock a target airborne.

How to Obtain

Defeat the Snake Doctor world boss in the Qinghe cave route to receive the tome.

Build Use

Useful for Silkbind - Jade setups that routinely create airborne/control windows before continuing ranged pressure.
