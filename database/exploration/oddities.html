Oddities

Regional collectibles that contribute to exploration progression and unlock rewards.

Why Collect Them

Oddities are tied to regional progression and can be reported to the appropriate NPC for rewards. Qinghe's Oddity route is also connected to the Bronze Bell Fragment and Bell of Demoncalm progression.

Tip

Collect them as you move through a region rather than leaving all cleanup for the end; many sit near other points of interest.
