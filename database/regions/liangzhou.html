Liangzhou

A later western campaign area tied to Version 1.5, new bosses, sect content and Martial Paths.

Overview

Liangzhou continues the Hexi journey with its own campaign, settlements, bosses and progression systems.

Major Content

  • Liangzhou Melody story content
  • Paper Moon story content
  • Raging Tides sect
  • Snowparting Blade and Phalanxbane Blade path content
  • Moongazing Maiden, Everdeer and Pocketrupt Circus world bosses
  • Wucan campaign encounter

Preparation

Players entering Liangzhou should have a settled Martial Path, upgraded gear and enough survivability for later-region bosses and exploration.
