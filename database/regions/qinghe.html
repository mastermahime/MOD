Region

Qinghe

The first major open region, built around rural settlements, mountains, monasteries and early exploration systems.

Overview

Qinghe introduces Boundary Stones, Wayfarers, Oddities, world bosses, side activities and many of the systems used throughout the rest of the game.

Important Areas

  • Verdant Wilds
  • Sundara Land
  • Moonveil Mountain
  • Harvestfall Village
  • Mercyheart Monastery
  • Heaven's Pier

World Bosses

Puppeteer - Sheng Wu, Sleeping Daoist, Puppeteer - Curtaincall, Earth Fiend Deity, Snake Doctor, Yi Dao and Coffin Master are among the Qinghe world-boss encounters.

What to Do Here

Activate Boundary Stones, report Oddities to Qi Sheng, unlock Mystic Skills, complete Jianghu Legacy quests and clear regional bosses before moving deeper into the campaign.
