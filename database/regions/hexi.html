Region

Hexi

The western desert region introduced through the Hexi content line, with sandstorms, caravan routes and new bosses.

Overview

Hexi shifts exploration into desert terrain and connects directly into the Liangzhou campaign content.

Key Areas

  • Shifting Sands
  • Jade Gate Pass
  • Lost Crossing
  • Jade-Mirrored Spring
  • Steed's Pass
  • Clear-Dew Terrace

Major Encounters

Wandering Ark, Moongazing Maiden, Pocketrupt Circus and Everdeer are major western-region world bosses.

Exploration

Expect visibility hazards, long traversal routes, new NPC chains and region-specific exploration objectives.
