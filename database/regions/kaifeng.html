Region

Kaifeng

A dense urban and surrounding region tied to the Universal Furnace chapter and many later combat systems.

Overview

Kaifeng expands the campaign into a busier city-and-outskirts structure with new world bosses, factions, quests, activities and stronger progression requirements.

Important Areas

  • Granary of Plenty
  • Jadewood Court
  • Roaring Sands
  • Grand Canal
  • Fairgrounds
  • Mistveil Forest

World Bosses

Wolf Maiden, Twin Lions, Ghost Master, Nameless General, Feng Ruzhi and Drunk Martial Artist are major world-boss encounters associated with Kaifeng.

Story

The region anchors Chapter 2, Universal Furnace, including New Guest in Kaifeng, Gathering of Heroes, Reunion, Accident and Furnace of Righteousness.
