Version 2.0–2.1 Region

Hidden Mountain

The centerpiece of the 2.0 era: a vast Mohist-themed mountain region mixing wuxia exploration with clockwork mechanisms.

Overview

Hidden Mountain marks the transition into Version 2.0 and later Clouded Revelation content. The region emphasizes vertical exploration, mechanisms, Mohist history and newer combat progression.

Version 2.1

Version 2.1 expands the region's connections and systems around Mohist Hill, including additional Martial Path, Inner Way, equipment and world-content progression.
