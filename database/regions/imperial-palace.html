Imperial Palace

A major high-level area introduced in Version 1.7, centered on palace exploration and story progression.

Overview

The Imperial Palace opened with Version 1.7 and added new quests, Oddities, map exploration and high-level progression content.

Systems

  • New palace map and story objectives
  • New Oddities and exploration collectibles
  • Path and development progression updates
  • Additional NPC and quest content

Tips

Activate map traversal points first, then clear story gates before attempting full collectible cleanup. The palace has received multiple post-launch fixes, so older route guides may not exactly match the current client.
