Martial Path · Flexible Melee DPS

Bellstrike - Splendor

BS

A flexible melee Martial Path built around movement, charged pressure and dependable single-target damage. The Sword gives quick repositioning and smooth pressure while the Spear adds reach and heavier burst, making the pair easy to use in a wide range of encounters.

Related Weapons: Nameless Sword and Nameless Spear.

Strengths

  • DPS
  • Mobility
  • Flexibility

Weaknesses

  • Support
  • Group Sustain
  • Heavy Control

Martial Arts

The two Bellstrike - Splendor weapons cover different melee ranges while sharing a straightforward damage-focused identity.

NS

Nameless Sword

Sword · Melee DPS / Mobility

A quick starter Sword that stays close to targets, repositions easily and keeps pressure consistent.

SS
Shadow Step

A mobility-focused technique used to reposition and stay connected to a target during melee pressure.

FL
Fearless Lunge

A committed forward attack that helps the Sword close distance and convert openings into damage.

NP

Nameless Spear

Spear · Melee DPS / Reach

The longer-range half of the pair, trading some Sword fluidity for reach, spacing and stronger committed hits.

LB
Legion Breaker

A heavy Spear pressure tool used to punish openings from a safer melee distance.

QL
Qiankun's Lock

A control-oriented Spear technique that helps secure space and keep targets in range for follow-up attacks.

Same Path, different skillsets: Nameless Sword is faster and more mobile, while Nameless Spear emphasizes reach and heavier commitment. Their Talents and individual skills should be evaluated separately.
