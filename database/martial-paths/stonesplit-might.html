Stonesplit - Might

SM

A defensive frontline Martial Path focused on surviving pressure, controlling space and staying committed in melee. Stormbreaker Spear provides steadier defensive reach while Thundercry Blade brings shields, trading and heavy committed attacks.

Related Weapons: Stormbreaker Spear and Thundercry Blade.

Strengths

  • Survivability
  • Frontline Control
  • Stability

Weaknesses

  • Mobility
  • Burst DPS
  • Ranged Pressure
