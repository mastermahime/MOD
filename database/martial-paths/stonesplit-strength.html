Stonesplit - Strength

SS

A melee Martial Path built around deflection, Blade Momentum and converting a successful setup into heavy damage. Snowparting Blade handles the faster parry-driven side of the rotation, while Phalanxbane Blade spends the opening on slower charged finishers.

Related Weapons: Snowparting Blade and Phalanxbane Blade.

Strengths

  • Burst
  • Deflection
  • Boss Pressure

Weaknesses

  • Mobility
  • Ranged Pressure
  • Support

Martial Arts

Stonesplit - Strength rewards correct timing. Snowparting creates momentum and openings; Phalanxbane turns those openings into heavier damage.

Snowparting Blade

Heng Blade · Parry Pressure / DPS

The faster setup weapon, rewarding successful deflections and building Blade Momentum for the paired heavy weapon.

Blade Momentum

Phalanxbane Blade

The cash-out weapon, using slower charged attacks to turn stored momentum and openings into large finishers.

BH

A multi-stage charged technique that can spend Blade Momentum for a stronger leap-and-slam finisher.

CF
Charged Finishers

Phalanxbane's core identity: commit to slower attacks when the target is controlled or a safe opening has been created.

Same Path, different skillsets: Snowparting is the parry and momentum engine; Phalanxbane is the heavy spender. Their individual Talents decide how each half of the rotation develops.
