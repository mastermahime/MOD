Bamboocut - Wind

BW

An aggressive Martial Path built around speed, chase pressure and staying on top of targets. Infernal Twinblades provide relentless melee strings while Mortal Rope Dart supplies pulls, gap closing and pet-assisted pressure.

Related Weapons: Infernal Twinblades and Mortal Rope Dart.

Strengths

  • DPS
  • Mobility
  • Chase

Weaknesses

  • Survivability
  • Support
  • Long-Range Safety

Martial Arts

Bamboocut - Wind is strongest when it keeps attacking. Rope Dart creates contact and Twinblades convert that contact into sustained melee pressure.

IT

Infernal Twinblades

Dual Blades · Fast Melee DPS

A high-speed melee style for players who want long attack strings and constant pressure.

CG
Calamity's Greed

A rapid multi-hit Twinblades technique used to keep damage flowing during close-range pressure.

AM
Addled Mind

A mobile spinning attack that helps the Twinblades stay attached to moving targets and continue a combo.

FL
Flamelash

A high-risk offensive state that converts the weapon's resource into stronger aggression at the cost of added danger.

MR

Mortal Rope Dart

Rope Dart · Control / Chase

A flexible Rope Dart that pulls normal enemies, pulls you toward bosses and adds summon pressure to the path.

BT
Bladebound Thread

Throws the Rope Dart to create contact: regular targets can be pulled toward you while boss hits pull you into melee range.

RR
Rodent Rampage

Summons a rodent that assists your attacks, adding extra pressure that can remain useful after a weapon swap.

Same Path, different skillsets: Twinblades are the direct damage engine; Mortal Rope Dart is the chase, pull and setup tool. Their Talents and individual skill timing remain separate.
