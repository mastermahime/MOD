Martial Path · Healing / Support

Silkbind - Deluge

SD

A fully supportive Martial Path focused on buffing both you and your allies with healing and damage bonuses. With this Martial Path, you can heal in an area, heal yourself or an ally, revive them if they get knocked down, and provide damage buffs while you wait for your healing abilities to come off cooldown.

Related Weapons: Panacea Fan and Soulshade Umbrella.

Strengths

  • Support
  • Survivability

Weaknesses

  • Control
  • DPS
  • Mobility

Martial Arts

For the Silkbind - Deluge Path, the Martial Art weapons and their associated skills are detailed below.

PF

Panacea Fan

Fan · Direct Healing / Support

The direct-healing side of Silkbind - Deluge, built for active recovery, emergency healing and keeping allies stable while remaining at a safer range.

FI
Fourfold Inquiry

The Fan's core sequence, used between larger support actions to keep the weapon active and maintain a smooth healing rotation.

ED
Emerald Dewtouch

A focused recovery tool suited to stabilizing yourself or a single ally when direct healing is needed.

CH
Cloudburst Healing

An area-oriented healing option that helps recover multiple nearby allies during group pressure.

LD
Light Dust After Morning Rain

A major support technique used as part of Panacea Fan's stronger recovery toolkit.

SU

Soulshade Umbrella

Umbrella · Sustained Support / Protection

The sustained-support side of the path, using ranged umbrella effects to maintain recovery, protection and team utility while Panacea Fan handles more direct healing.

FG
Floating Grace

A mobile umbrella technique that helps maintain support pressure while repositioning around the fight.

PP
Pale Petal

A support-focused technique that contributes to Soulshade Umbrella's protective and restorative playstyle.

EP
Echoes of a Thousand Plants

A signature sustained-support effect for keeping allies healthy while the Umbrella remains active at range.

Weapon skillsets are different even inside the same Martial Path. Panacea Fan and Soulshade Umbrella share Silkbind - Deluge's support identity, but each has its own attacks, skills and Talents. Treat the pair as two complementary toolkits rather than identical versions of the same weapon.
