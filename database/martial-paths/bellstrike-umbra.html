Bellstrike - Umbra

BU

A pressure-focused Martial Path that builds damage through wounds, repeated hits and setup before converting that pressure into stronger payoff windows. Strategies Sword handles deliberate setup while Heavenquaker Spear adds reach, movement and continued wound pressure.

Related Weapons: Strategies Sword and Heavenquaker Spear.

Strengths

  • DPS
  • Sustained Pressure
  • Boss Damage

Weaknesses

  • Healing
  • Immediate Defense
  • Setup Time

Martial Arts

Bellstrike - Umbra rewards players who maintain pressure instead of relying only on isolated burst attacks.

Strategies Sword

Sword · Bleed Setup / Melee DPS

A deliberate Sword style that rewards preparing a target before cashing out the setup with stronger follow-up pressure.

TR
Third Track Recoil

A setup-oriented Sword technique used to continue pressure and build toward the path's stronger damage windows.

IT
Inner Track Slash

A follow-up attack that fits the Strategies Sword's wound-focused rotation and rewards maintaining contact.

HS

Heavenquaker Spear

Spear · Wound Pressure / Reach

A mobile Spear that extends Bellstrike - Umbra's pressure with longer reach and aggressive repositioning.

SA
Sweep All

A broad Spear pressure tool for keeping enemies inside your attack range and maintaining momentum.

SO
Sober Sorrow

A committed Spear technique used as part of the path's sustained wound and damage cycle.

Same Path, different skillsets: Strategies Sword emphasizes setup and conversion, while Heavenquaker Spear is more mobile and reach-oriented. Their Talents and rotations are not interchangeable.
