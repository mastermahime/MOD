Bamboocut - Draught

BDR

A close-range Martial Path built around a shared drinking resource. Skystrike Gauntlets help build Binge Points and enter stronger Inebriate states, while Riven Twinblades convert those drunken windows into fast slashing pressure and stronger payoff attacks.

Related Weapons: Skystrike Gauntlets and Riven Twinblades.

Strengths

  • DPS
  • Burst
  • Mobility

Weaknesses

  • Support
  • Resource Management
  • Setup Dependence

Martial Arts

Bamboocut - Draught is designed as one shared drunken-state system. The Gauntlets build and shape the meter; the Twinblades use that state for faster, more aggressive damage sequences.

SG

Skystrike Gauntlets

Gauntlets · Melee DPS / Resource Builder

The drunken-fist half of the path, using drinking windows and close-range attacks to build Binge Points and prepare Inebriate-enhanced actions.

DR
Drinking Heavy Attack

Holding the heavy input drinks and builds the shared gauge, trading time and stamina for faster access to the path's stronger drunken states.

FS
Falcon Strike Timing

Well-timed quick drinking after certain attacks can trigger an extra payoff, rewarding players who learn the Gauntlets' rhythm instead of drinking at random.

IN
Inebriate-Enhanced Attacks

Once the shared state is active, parts of the Gauntlet kit become stronger and feed naturally into the paired Twinblades rotation.

Riven Twinblades

Dual Blades · Melee DPS / Drunken Payoff

The faster payoff half of the pair, using the shared drunken state for rapid slashes, aggressive engagement and stronger Inebriate sequences.

SD
Special Dash

A fast engagement tool that can contribute to the shared meter when it connects, making it a natural opener into the Twinblades sequence.

HS
Hold-Slash Sequence

A sustained slashing action that accelerates sharply during the stronger drunken state and serves as a major damage payoff.

BW
Inebriate Blade Wave

At peak drunken state, the Twinblades gain a stronger special sequence that extends pressure with a wide multi-hit payoff.

Same Path, different skillsets: Skystrike Gauntlets are the meter-building drunken-fist side; Riven Twinblades are the faster spender. Both use Binge Points, but their individual attacks and Talents play very differently.
