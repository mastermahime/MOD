Martial Path · Control into Burst

Bamboocut - Kite

BK

A two-weapon Martial Path built around Heaven's Will. Skygrasp Rope Dart opens fights by controlling targets and applying setup effects, then Heavenwill Gauntlets take over at close range to build resource and cash it out through powerful charged punches.

Related Weapons: Heavenwill Gauntlets and Skygrasp Rope Dart.

Strengths

  • Burst
  • Control
  • Mobility

Weaknesses

  • Healing
  • Resource Management
  • Missed Burst Windows

Martial Arts

Bamboocut - Kite works best when the Rope Dart prepares the target before the Gauntlets spend the resulting Heaven's Will on burst damage.

HG

Heavenwill Gauntlets

Gauntlets · Close-Range Burst

The damage half of the path, using repeated pressure and Perfect Dodge follow-ups to generate Heaven's Will before spending it on finishers.

RR
Righteous Reign

The core light-attack chain. Completing the later hits can trigger Falcon's Pursuit and help restore Heaven's Will.

WD
Wicked Defiance

A Perfect-Dodge follow-up that closes distance, keeps pressure active and contributes to Heaven's Will generation.

VC
Vile Condemned

The major Heaven's Will spender and one of the Gauntlets' defining burst attacks.

FP
Falcon's Pursuit

A recurring Martial Trigger Effect that contributes damage and resource generation across the path's rotation.

SR

Skygrasp Rope Dart

Rope Dart · Setup / Control

The setup half of the path, applying control and creating better Heaven's Will generation before the swap into Gauntlets.

BW
Boundless Whirl

A multi-hit heavy attack that can consume Heaven's Will for stronger range and damage when the situation allows.

SL
Snaring Lash

The Rope Dart's primary engagement skill, combining movement, knockdown pressure and important setup for the Gauntlets.

SS
Sky-Snare Stance

Pulls and gathers enemies, then improves Heaven's Will generation so the following Gauntlet rotation reaches its payoff faster.

SV
Soaring Stance: Sever

A situational boss perception finisher that adds a strong PvE payoff when its conditions are met.

Same Path, different skillsets: Skygrasp Rope Dart creates the opening; Heavenwill Gauntlets exploit it. Their separate Talents strengthen different parts of the same Heaven's Will rotation.
