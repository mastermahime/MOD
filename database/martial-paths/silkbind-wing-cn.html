Silkbind - Wing (CN)

A support-DPS Martial Path built around Ribbon & Drum team buffs, crowd control and a paired Qingzhou Fan with additional damage, control and light recovery.
CN Server: This Martial Path is currently China-server content and is not yet available on Global. English names on this page are guide labels until an official Global localization is released.
翼

Silkbind - Wing is a team-oriented support path that combines group damage amplification, protection and crowd control with strong area pressure. Ribbon & Drum handles the path's signature buffs and control, while the paired Fan adds mobility, zone control and extra offensive utility.

Related Weapons: Ribbon & Drum (CN) and Qingzhou Fan (CN).

Strengths

  • Team Support
  • Crowd Control
  • AoE Pressure
  • Mobility

Weaknesses

  • Less efficient as a pure solo DPS path
  • Some control loses value against control-resistant bosses
  • Strongest effects depend on maintaining the path's resource loop

Martial Arts

鼓

Ribbon & Drum (CN)

舞绫鼓 · CN Martial Art: 文动霓裳

The main support weapon of the path. Ribbon attacks can strike and restrain enemies, while the drum side provides team-oriented buffs, protection and control effects.

扇

Qingzhou Fan (CN)

Fan · CN Martial Art: 杏游草野

The paired Fan style adds zone control, mobility and offensive support. It can contribute light recovery, but the path is primarily a buffer/controller rather than a replacement for Silkbind - Deluge healing.

How the Pair Plays

Use Ribbon & Drum to establish the team's support window and control the fight, then rotate into Fan when you need movement, extra area pressure or a different control angle. The pair is strongest in group content where its buffs and control benefit multiple allies at once.
