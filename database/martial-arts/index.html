Martial Arts

Weapon styles, Martial Paths, recommended pairings and unlock methods. Each style has its own editable page.
How this database is organized: weapon ownership and Martial Art mastery are related but not identical. Many styles are learned through Skill Theft, sect progression, quests or manuals. The paired styles below share a Martial Path and are usually designed to work together.

Choosing a Pair

Staying inside a Martial Path is the easiest way to get a coherent build because the two arts often share resources or trigger conditions. Hybrid weapon bars can still work when the second style solves a specific problem such as healing, range, defense or crowd control.
