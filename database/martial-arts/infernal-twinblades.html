Overview

An aggressive dual-blade style for long pressure strings, chase-heavy combat and high input speed.

Notable Skills / Mechanics

Calamity's Greed; Addled Mind

Recommended Pairing

This style is designed to work naturally with the other martial art on Bamboocut - Wind, though hybrid pairings can be useful when the second weapon fills a clear role such as healing, control, range or defense.

How to Obtain

Steal the art in Midnight Mercy Sanctum, learn it through Midnight Mercy, or receive it from Qi Sheng after the first Oddity.
