Overview

A slow, heavy defensive style built around shields, trading and large committed attacks.

Notable Skills / Mechanics

Sunrush Gale; Predator's Shield / Shifting Heaven

Recommended Pairing

This style is designed to work naturally with the other martial art on Stonesplit - Might, though hybrid pairings can be useful when the second weapon fills a clear role such as healing, control, range or defense.

How to Obtain

Steal it in Well of Heaven Martial Camp, learn it through Well of Heaven, or choose it from Qi Sheng after the first Oddity.
