Inkwell Fan

A tactical ranged DPS fan built around projectile pressure, wind barriers, airborne setups and empowered pursuit attacks.
Weapon TypeFan
Martial PathSilkbind - Jade
RoleRanged DPS / Control
Core MechanicLaunch or mark targets, gain Windrider, then cash out with Moon Shatter Spring.
How to UnlockUse Skill Theft inside Inkwell Fan Sanctum in northern Qinghe. A Dragon Key is required to open the hidden entrance.

Overview

Inkwell Fan fights from mid range, using fan projectiles to pressure enemies while controlling how they approach. Its strongest loop is built around Launch, Lingering Bone, Windrider and Enhanced Ballistics. Jadewind Shield creates breathing room and buffs projectile pressure, while Peak's Springless Silence and Forsaken Fame set up targets for Moon Shatter Spring.

Combat kit

Core Skills

Peak's Springless Silence

Special Skill · ~12s

Dash to the target and briefly immobilize it. Humanoid targets are launched; non-player enemies receive Lingering Bone. Hitting an already launched or marked target grants Windrider. The cast also leaves a Water Clone that you can return to within 5 seconds.

Input: Special Skill / ~ key

Jadewind Shield

Martial Skill · ~15s

Knocks back nearby enemies, jumps you backward and creates an 8-second wind barrier. The barrier blocks non-boss units and enemy projectiles. Casting it also gives a short evasive window and stacks Enhanced Ballistics up to 2 times.

Input: Q

Fan - Light Attack / Forsaken Fame

Basic / Charged

Tap for a four-hit ranged fan string. Hold to use Forsaken Fame, sending a whirlwind forward that launches humanoids or applies Lingering Bone to non-player enemies, setting up your pursuit heavy.

Input: Left Click / Hold Left Click

Fan - Heavy / Moon Shatter Spring

Heavy / Follow-up

Heavy Attack chains up to three grounded strikes. Against a launched or Lingering Bone target, trigger Moon Shatter Spring for an aerial pursuit. Windrider upgrades it from a three-hit sequence to a stronger five-hit sequence; non-player enemies take additional pursuit damage.

Input: R · Follow-up R when prompted

Glow & Flow

Charged Skill

Briefly charge, then surge forward and hit enemies ahead twice. Useful for closing a short gap or continuing pressure after a ranged setup.

Input: Hold R

Fan - Conversion

Dual-Weapon Skill

Swap into the Inkwell Fan with an attacking slash. This is the natural transition back into Fan setup after spending a Vernal Umbrella damage window.

Input: Weapon Swap / Tab

Simple Combat Loop

  1. Cast Jadewind Shield to create space and activate Enhanced Ballistics.
  2. Use Forsaken Fame or Peak's Springless Silence to Launch or mark the target.
  3. Hit an already launched/marked target with Peak's Springless Silence when possible to gain Windrider.
  4. Trigger Moon Shatter Spring from Heavy Attack and spend Windrider on the empowered aerial sequence.
  5. Reset to range, refresh Jadewind Shield, or swap to Vernal Umbrella for projectile pressure.

Recommended Pairing

Vernal Umbrella is the natural partner because both weapons belong to Silkbind - Jade. Inkwell Fan handles barriers, launches and setup while Vernal Umbrella converts the projectile-damage window into sustained ranged pressure.

How to Obtain

Travel to Inkwell Fan Sanctum in northern Qinghe. Use a Dragon Key at the Loong Herm Dragon Pillar to open the hidden route, move through the sanctum, then perform Skill Theft on the master in the innermost chamber to learn the martial art.

The sanctum also contains exploration rewards and the Flying Gourds Inner Way, which directly supports Peak's Springless Silence.
