A ranged fan style built around spacing, projectile pressure and control from safer distances.

Recommended Pairing

This style is designed to work naturally with the other martial art on Silkbind - Jade, though hybrid pairings can be useful when the second weapon fills a clear role such as healing, control, range or defense.
