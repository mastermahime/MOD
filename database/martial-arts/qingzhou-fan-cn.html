Overview

The Qingzhou Fan is the second half of the CN Silkbind - Wing pair. It adds mobile attacks, area control and offensive support while Ribbon & Drum handles the path's strongest team-buff and control windows.

Core Mechanics

  • Zone control: several actions are designed to influence space around the target rather than only dealing straight-line damage.
  • Mobility: the Fan helps reposition during the path's support cycle and gives the pair a more active movement option.
  • Support DPS: the style contributes meaningful damage while still reinforcing the path's group-focused identity.
  • Light recovery: the Fan can provide some recovery utility, but it is not a replacement for a dedicated Silkbind - Deluge healer.

How to Obtain

CN Server only. The Martial Art is part of Qingzhou's 牵丝·翊 content. Global acquisition will be added when the weapon receives an official Global release.
