Overview

Ribbon & Drum is the signature support weapon of the CN Silkbind - Wing path. Ribbon attacks can strike and restrain enemies, while the drum side is used to create strong team-oriented effects such as damage amplification, protection, movement support and crowd control.

Core Mechanics

  • Ribbon pressure: flexible attacks that can reach, bind and disrupt targets.
  • Drum support states: special drum effects provide different offensive, defensive or control-oriented benefits.
  • Team amplification: the weapon is designed to make the group stronger instead of functioning as a pure personal-DPS weapon.
  • Control value: especially strong in PvP and group encounters where crowd control matters.

How to Obtain

CN Server only. The weapon is tied to Qingzhou content and the 牵丝·翊 Martial Path. Global acquisition will be added when the weapon receives an official Global release.
