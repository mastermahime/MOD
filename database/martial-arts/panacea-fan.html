Overview

The direct-healing half of the main support path, offering safe ranged sustain and recovery for solo or group play.

Notable Skills / Mechanics

Light Dust After Morning Rain; Mistbound Aid

Recommended Pairing

This style is designed to work naturally with the other martial art on Silkbind - Deluge, though hybrid pairings can be useful when the second weapon fills a clear role such as healing, control, range or defense.

How to Obtain

Steal it in Silver Needle Sanctum, learn it through Silver Needle, or choose it from Qi Sheng after the first Oddity.
