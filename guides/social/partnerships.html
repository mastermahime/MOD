Social System

Partnerships

Two-player relationship progression with co-op rewards and an exclusive Mystic Skill.

Benefits

  • Dedicated Partnership progression and shared milestones
  • Co-op/social rewards as Partnership Level increases
  • Exclusive Paired Radiance Mystic Skill at Partnership Level 3
  • Additional reasons to run compatible co-op activities together

Paired Radiance

When grouped with your partner in supported content, Paired Radiance enables a cooperative teleport/performance effect.
