Core Weapons

Stormbreaker SpearThundercry Blade

Default Martial Path

Stonesplit - Might is the frontline path, using Spear for reach and space control while Thundercry Blade handles heavier defensive trades.

Recommended Inner Ways

Art of ResistanceExquisite SceneryRock Solid

Gear Focus

Prioritize HP, Physical Defense and damage reduction before adding offense. The path gains value by staying present through pressure instead of racing for short burst windows.

Suggested Mystic Skills

Guardian PalmGolden BodyCloud StepsTai Chi

How to Play

  1. Use Stormbreaker Spear to hold space and pressure from safer reach.
  2. Swap to Thundercry Blade when you expect a committed trade or dangerous enemy string.
  3. Use defensive Mystic Skills before your HP reaches an emergency state.
  4. Return to Spear when you need safer reach or more flexible control.
