Core Weapons

Default Martial Path

Silkbind - Jade focuses on ranged pressure, movement and controlling the space between you and the target.

Recommended Inner Ways

Blossom BarrageFlying GourdsStar ReacherThunderous Bloom

Gear Focus

Favor ranged damage and Affinity while keeping enough mobility or defense to preserve your preferred distance. This build performs best when enemies are forced to approach on your terms.

Suggested Mystic Skills

Dragon's BreathCloud StepsTai ChiGolden Body

How to Play

  1. Open from range and make the enemy cross space before it can threaten you.
  2. Use Inkwell Fan control when opponents push directly toward you.
  3. Swap to Vernal Umbrella while repositioning or maintaining ranged pressure.
  4. Use mobility before enemies fully collapse your spacing.
