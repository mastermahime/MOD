Bamboocut - Dust is built around deliberate swaps: Everspring Umbrella creates the opening and Unfettered Rope Dart converts it into mobile burst.

Gear Focus

How to Play

  1. Use Everspring Umbrella to create pressure, grouping or a safe opening.
  2. Wait until the target is positioned well instead of swapping immediately.
  3. Swap into Unfettered Rope Dart for the burst sequence.
  4. Return to Umbrella after the payoff window instead of staying in the wrong range.
