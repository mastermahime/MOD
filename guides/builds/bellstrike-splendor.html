Bellstrike - Splendor Build

Nameless Sword + Nameless Spear.

Core Weapons

Nameless SwordNameless Spear

Default Martial Path

Bellstrike - Splendor is a flexible melee path that uses Sword for fast pressure and Spear for reach, spacing and safer punish windows.

Recommended Inner Ways

Battle AnthemMountain's MightSword MorphWildfire Spark

Gear Focus

Favor reliable Physical Attack and Affinity value with enough defense to keep attacking through normal pressure. This build works best when its damage is consistent rather than tied to a narrow trigger.

Suggested Mystic Skills

Dragon's BreathCloud StepsTai ChiGolden Body

How to Play

  1. Use Nameless Sword for general pressure and quick repositioning.
  2. Swap to Nameless Spear when reach or spacing is more valuable than staying directly on the target.
  3. Use control and mobility Mystic Skills to create safe damage windows.
  4. Return to Sword once you can safely stay close again.
