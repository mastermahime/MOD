Bellstrike - Splendor Build

Nameless Sword + Nameless Spear.

Core Weapons

Nameless Sword and Nameless Spear.

Build Goal

Use Sword for flexible pressure and Spear for reach and control. This is one of the easiest paths to learn because it covers many situations without a narrow gimmick.

Gear & Inner Ways

Favor consistent Physical Attack/Affinity value and Inner Ways that improve reliable damage rather than requiring a trigger you rarely activate.
