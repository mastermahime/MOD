Core Weapons

Default Martial Path

Silkbind - Deluge is the dedicated healing and support path. Panacea Fan handles direct recovery while Soulshade Umbrella maintains support between larger heals.

Recommended Inner Ways

Esoteric RevivalMending LoomRestoring BlossomRoyal Remedy

Gear Focus

Favor healing output, survivability and support uptime. Staying alive and keeping your major recovery tools available is more important than maximizing personal DPS.

Suggested Mystic Skills

World to SwordCyclone SpinCloud StepsGuardian Palm

How to Play

  1. Keep Soulshade Umbrella support active before the team takes heavy pressure.
  2. Swap to Panacea Fan for focused healing or emergency recovery.
  3. Return to Umbrella while larger healing tools recover.
  4. Keep a mobility option available so you can survive dives or reach an ally quickly.
