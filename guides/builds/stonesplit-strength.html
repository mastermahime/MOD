How to Play

  1. Use Snowparting Blade to pressure safely and build momentum through deflection.
  2. Wait for a real recovery or punish window before switching to the heavy weapon.
  3. Use Phalanxbane Blade to cash out the momentum with a committed finisher.
  4. Reset back into Snowparting Blade once the heavy sequence ends.
