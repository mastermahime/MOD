Core Weapons

Default Martial Path

Bellstrike - Umbra builds pressure through wound and bleed setup before converting that setup into stronger payoff windows.

Recommended Inner Ways

Adaptive SteelInsightful StrikeSword HorizonWolfchaser's Art

Gear Focus

Favor sustained offensive stats, penetration and enough survivability to keep your damage-over-time pressure active. The build loses value if you repeatedly reset before reaching its payoff.

Suggested Mystic Skills

Dragon's BreathCloud StepsTai ChiGolden Body

How to Play

  1. Start with Strategies Sword to establish wound or bleed pressure.
  2. Maintain the setup instead of swapping too early.
  3. Use Heavenquaker Spear when reach is safer or when the target tries to disengage.
  4. Commit to your payoff after the setup is established.
