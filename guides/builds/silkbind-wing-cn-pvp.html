Silkbind - Wing (CN) PvP

Ribbon & Drum + Qingzhou Fan.
CN Server: This build uses China-server content and is not currently available on Global.

Weapons

Ribbon & Drum (CN)Qingzhou Fan (CN)

Martial Path

Silkbind - Wing (CN) / 牵丝·翊

Build Priorities

  • Keep team damage amplification available for coordinated pushes.
  • Use crowd control to interrupt enemy momentum instead of spending every support tool on damage.
  • Preserve mobility so you can reposition after committing a long Ribbon & Drum animation.
  • Stay alive long enough to cycle multiple support windows.

Common CN Inner Ways

易水歌相和歌弦墨篇寸草心

Gear Focus

Use the CN 浣花 set as the main starting point and value survivability alongside support uptime. PvP performance depends heavily on completing your buff and control windows without being interrupted.

How to Play

  1. Enter the fight from a position where Ribbon & Drum can affect multiple teammates or enemies.
  2. Use control when the opposing team commits, then activate the support state that best fits the exchange.
  3. Swap to Fan for movement, follow-up pressure and zone control.
  4. Disengage briefly when needed so your next support window is available for the next team push.
