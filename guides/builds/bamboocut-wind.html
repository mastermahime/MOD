Bamboocut - Wind Build

Core Weapons

Infernal TwinbladesMortal Rope Dart

Default Martial Path

Bamboocut - Wind is a high-tempo chase path that uses Rope Dart to establish contact and Twinblades to maintain fast melee pressure.

Recommended Inner Ways

Breaking PointEchoes of OblivionRiptide ReflexVendetta

Gear Focus

Favor offensive stats that reward staying on the target, then add enough survivability to handle the close-range commitment this path requires.

Suggested Mystic Skills

Dragon's BreathCloud StepsTai ChiGolden Body

How to Play

  1. Use Mortal Rope Dart to approach, group or stop an enemy from freely escaping.
  2. Swap into Infernal Twinblades when you have a clean opening.
  3. Keep at least one mobility or defensive option available instead of spending everything at once.
  4. Return to Rope Dart if the target escapes your effective melee range.
