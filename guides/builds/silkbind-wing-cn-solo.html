Silkbind - Wing (CN) Solo

Ribbon & Drum + Qingzhou Fan.
CN Server: This build uses China-server content and is not currently available on Global.

Weapons

Ribbon & Drum (CN)Qingzhou Fan (CN)

Martial Path

Silkbind - Wing (CN) / 牵丝·翊

Build Priorities

  • Favor damage and self-sufficiency more than full group support.
  • Use Ribbon & Drum control to group or disable normal enemies.
  • Use Fan more aggressively for movement and area damage.
  • Keep one defensive support state available for elites and bosses.

Common CN Inner Ways

易水歌相和歌弦墨篇寸草心

Gear Focus

The CN 浣花 set remains a strong starting point. For solo play, lean harder into damage while keeping enough survivability to finish the longer Ribbon & Drum animations safely.

How to Play

  1. Open with Ribbon control to gather or disrupt enemies.
  2. Use the offensive support state when you can safely complete the animation.
  3. Swap to Fan to chase, reposition and continue area pressure.
  4. Use the defensive side of Ribbon & Drum when fighting elites or bosses instead of forcing damage through dangerous strings.
