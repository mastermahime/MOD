Default Martial Path

Silkbind - Wing (CN) / 牵丝·翊 is a support-DPS path focused on team damage amplification, crowd control, protection and AoE pressure.

These are shown with their CN names until official Global localization is available.

Gear Focus

The CN 浣花 gear set is commonly used with this path. Prioritize support uptime and enough survivability to stay active through the full buff cycle.

Build Goal

Keep the team's amplification and protection active, use Ribbon & Drum for the strongest support/control windows, then rotate into Fan for movement, area pressure and continued utility.

How to Play

  1. Build or prepare the Ribbon & Drum resource before the team commits to a damage window.
  2. Use the appropriate drum state for offense, defense or control.
  3. Swap to Qingzhou Fan while the support effects are active to maintain pressure and mobility.
  4. Return to Ribbon & Drum as the next team-buff window becomes available.
