PvE Endgame

Heroes' Realm

High-level PvE boss content with rotating or updated encounter sets.

What Matters

Learn mechanics first, then optimize damage. Heroes' Realm has included encounters such as Twin Lions and Ghost Master in later level bands.

Preparation

Use mode-specific buff auto-use if desired, bring a full build, and coordinate utility rather than stacking only damage.
