Perception Forest

Battle-royale-style PvP activity tied to sect progression and Severance Trials.

Uses

Perception Forest is especially relevant to Midnight Blades Karmic progression and can be required to finish a Severance Trial after leaving certain sects.

Build Advice

Use a PvP loadout with mobility and target access. A pure bossing build can struggle when opponents disengage or control you.
