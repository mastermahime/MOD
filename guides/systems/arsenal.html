Progression System

Arsenal

Long-term account progression that converts collected/raised equipment into persistent attribute bonuses.

Current vs Previous Arsenal

The August 20, 2026 update split Arsenal progression into Current Arsenal and Previous Arsenal based on release timing. Previous Arsenal progress can reach its Attribute Attack and HP goals without relying on overflow, while Current Arsenal retains the newer overflow progression structure.

Why It Matters

Arsenal is account-wide long-term power. Do not treat every unusable gear drop as worthless before checking whether it contributes to Arsenal progress.

Priority

Keep your active combat gear first, then feed duplicate/obsolete progression into Arsenal according to the current interface recommendations.
