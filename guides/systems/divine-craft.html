Divine Craft

Late-game equipment crafting and targeted progression system.

Purpose

Divine Craft is part of late-game gear optimization, letting players target stronger equipment progression rather than relying only on random drops.

When to Invest

Wait until your Martial Path and intended gear-set family are settled. Spending high-tier crafting resources on a set you will replace wastes more progression than using temporarily imperfect gear.

Checklist

  1. Choose your Martial Path.
  2. Pick the offensive and defensive set effects you want.
  3. Confirm your current tier/season cap.
  4. Craft/tune only pieces that move you toward that target.
