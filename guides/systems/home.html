Home & Homestead

Housing, decorating, pets and companion-focused life content.

Overview

The Homestead system expanded substantially in the 1.8 Companions Make Home era, adding more persistent home/life content and animal-companion features.

What This Section Covers

  • Home and decoration progression
  • Pet/animal companion features
  • Life-skill interactions
  • Future furniture and housing unlock pages

Progression Tip

Treat Homestead as a separate long-term life track. It does not need to delay your main weapon/gear progression unless a home objective directly rewards resources you currently need.
